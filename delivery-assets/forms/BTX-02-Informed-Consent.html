About This Treatment

Neurotoxin injections temporarily relax targeted facial muscles by blocking nerve signals. Effects appear within 3–14 days and last approximately 3–4 months, varying by individual, product, treatment area, and dosage. Brand options include Botox®, Dysport®, Xeomin®, Daxxify®, and Jeuveau®.

Benefits
Temporary reduction of dynamic lines and wrinkles
Natural-looking softening of expression lines
Non-surgical with minimal downtime
Results typically visible within 3–14 days
Risks & Complications
Common — expected & temporaryRedness, swelling, bruising at injection sites · Mild headache · Temporary tenderness · Incomplete response requiring touch-up
Less commonAsymmetry · Resistance with repeat treatments · Delayed onset or shorter duration · Spread of toxin to adjacent muscles
Rare but seriousEyelid ptosis (drooping) · Brow ptosis or asymmetry · Diplopia (double vision) · Difficulty swallowing or speaking (extremely rare at cosmetic doses) · Allergic reaction
Contraindications
I confirm I do NOT have the following (or have disclosed them):
Pregnancy or breastfeeding
Known allergy to botulinum toxin or albumin
Active infection at treatment site
Neuromuscular disorder (MG, ALS, Lambert-Eaton)
Aminoglycosides or neuromuscular blockers
Alternatives

Alternatives include no treatment, topical skincare, chemical peels, laser resurfacing, microneedling, and surgical options. The risks and benefits of alternatives have been offered for discussion.
