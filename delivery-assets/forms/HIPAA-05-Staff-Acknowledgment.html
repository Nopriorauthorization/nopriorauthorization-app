Confidentiality Pledge

I understand that in the course of my duties at Hello Gorgeous Med Spa, I may have access to confidential patient information, including but not limited to medical records, treatment plans, billing information, photographs, and personal identifying information. I hereby pledge the following:

1. I will access only the minimum amount of PHI necessary to perform my assigned job duties. I will not access PHI for any other purpose, including curiosity, concern for a friend or family member, or personal interest.

2. I will not disclose PHI to any unauthorized person, whether inside or outside the Practice, including discussions in public areas, social media posts, text messages, or casual conversations with coworkers not involved in the patient's care.

3. I will safeguard all forms of PHI, whether oral, written, or electronic. I will log off workstations when unattended, store paper records securely, and follow all Practice policies related to information security.

4. I will immediately report any suspected or known breach of PHI, security incident, or policy violation to the Privacy Officer within 24 hours of discovery.

5. I understand that my obligation to protect PHI continues even after my employment or engagement with the Practice ends.

Understanding of Consequences
Sanctions for Violations I understand that violation of HIPAA regulations or Practice confidentiality policies may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. I further understand that violations of HIPAA may result in civil penalties of up to $50,000 per violation (with an annual maximum of $1,500,000 per violation category) and criminal penalties including fines up to $250,000 and imprisonment for up to 10 years for willful or malicious violations.
I have read and understand the sanctions described above and acknowledge that I am subject to disciplinary action, civil penalties, and criminal prosecution for HIPAA violations.
