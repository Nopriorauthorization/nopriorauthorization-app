About the Chemical Process

A lash lift (lash perm) uses a lifting solution and setting solution applied to your lashes. A tint uses semi-permanent dye. Both are chemical products that may cause reactions in sensitive individuals.

Possible risks include:

Temporary eye redness or irritation
Tint contact with skin (temporary staining)
Over-processing — lashes too curly or weakened
Allergic reaction to processing solutions or tint
Lash breakage if lashes are very fine or chemically treated
Contraindications

Please inform your technician if any of the following apply:

ConditionYesNo
Currently pregnant or breastfeeding
Known allergy to peroxide, ammonia, or hair dye
Recent eye surgery (within 6 months)
Active eye infection or stye
Very sparse, damaged, or extremely short natural lashes
Previous chemical lash service within 6 weeks
Currently undergoing chemotherapy
Results

Lash lift results last 6–8 weeks and gradually soften as natural lashes grow. A second lift should not be performed within 6–8 weeks. Results vary based on natural lash length, texture, and health.
