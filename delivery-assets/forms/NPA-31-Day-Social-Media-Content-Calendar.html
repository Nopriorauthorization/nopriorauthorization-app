Best Posting Times for Med Spa & Esthetic Audiences

When your audience is most active

  • Instagram: Tuesday–Friday, 9–11am and 6–8pm local time. Reels perform best when posted between 9am–12pm.
  • Facebook: Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–2pm. Facebook groups: anytime — post when you have something genuine to add.
  • TikTok: Tuesday, Thursday, Friday — 7–9am, 12–3pm, 7–11pm. Algorithm rewards completion rate over timing.
  • Google Business Profile: Post weekly — any day. These posts help local SEO regardless of engagement.
  • General rule: Post at the same times consistently. Your audience learns when to expect you — and the algorithm rewards predictability.

Bonus
Reels, Stories & Repurposing Guide
How to get 3x the content from every post you create
From Danielle
"Every piece of content I create gets used at least three times. A feed post becomes a Story. A Story becomes a Reel with trending audio. A Reel becomes a Google Business Profile post. You don't need 31 completely different ideas — you need 10 great ideas used strategically."
The Content Repurposing Formula

One post → 4 pieces of content

  • Feed post (static or carousel): The full caption with all the detail. This is your anchor content.
  • Stories: Share the feed post to Stories + add a poll or question sticker to drive engagement. "Did you know this? Yes / No" works every time.
  • Reel: Take the hook from your feed post and turn it into a talking head Reel (30–60 seconds). Same information, different format. Reels reach new audiences. Feed posts reach existing followers.
  • Google Business Profile post: Shorten the caption to 2–3 sentences + your booking link. Post weekly on GBP. This helps local SEO regardless of engagement. Takes 3 minutes.
