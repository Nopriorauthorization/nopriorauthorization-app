Dear [Patient Name],

We're looking forward to seeing you for your Botox appointment. To help you get the best possible results and the smoothest experience, please review the following instructions carefully before your visit. These guidelines are designed to minimize bruising, optimize results, and ensure your comfort.

1
48 Hours Before Your Appointment
🚫
Avoid blood thinners. Hold aspirin, ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin), naproxen (Aleve), and other NSAIDs for 48 hours prior — unless prescribed by your physician for a medical condition. Do not stop prescribed medications without consulting your doctor.
🚫
Pause these supplements. Fish oil, vitamin E, flaxseed oil, garlic, ginger, turmeric, and high-dose vitamin C can increase bleeding and bruising risk. Hold these for 48 hours prior if possible.
🍷
No alcohol 24 hours before. Alcohol is a vasodilator and increases bruising risk significantly. Avoid all alcohol the evening before and day of your appointment.
☀️
Avoid excessive sun exposure. Do not get a sunburn in the 24 hours before your appointment. Sunburned skin is contraindicated for injection.
💉
No other facial treatments. Avoid chemical peels, laser treatments, microneedling, or other facial procedures for 2 weeks before your Botox appointment.
2
Day of Your Appointment
🧴
Arrive with a clean face. Please arrive without makeup, moisturizer, or sunscreen on the treatment areas. A clean face allows us to work efficiently and maintain sterility.
🥗
Eat a light meal before your appointment. Having food in your stomach helps prevent lightheadedness. Do not come fasting.
💧
Stay well hydrated. Drink plenty of water the day of your appointment. Good hydration supports optimal skin condition.
👗
Wear comfortable clothing. Loose, comfortable clothing you don't mind creasing is ideal. Avoid tight collars or tops that are difficult to remove if needed.
⚠️
Let us know if you have any active skin infections, cold sores, or rashes near the planned treatment area. We may need to reschedule for your safety.
3
Helpful to Know Before You Arrive

✓ Good to do

  • Drink extra water the day before
  • Get a full night of sleep
  • Take arnica supplements (optional, may reduce bruising)
  • Apply ice to treatment area 10 min before if anxious about discomfort
  • Bring a list of all medications and supplements
  • Tell us about any recent illness or fever

✗ Please avoid

  • Blood thinning medications or supplements
  • Alcohol the night before
  • Tanning or sunburning
  • Strenuous exercise the morning of
  • Arriving with a full face of makeup
  • Scheduling within 2 weeks of a facial event

Questions before your appointment? Please don't hesitate to reach out to us at [Phone Number] or [Email]. We want you to feel completely prepared and at ease before you arrive. We can't wait to see you!

Description of Treatment

Botulinum toxin type A (Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau, or Daxxify) is a prescription medication injected into specific muscles to temporarily reduce the appearance of dynamic wrinkles caused by repetitive facial movement. Results typically appear within 3–7 days and last approximately 3–4 months. Individual results may vary.

Risks and Potential Complications

I understand that this procedure carries risks, which may include but are not limited to:

  • Temporary bruising, swelling, redness, or tenderness at injection sites
  • Headache — typically resolving within 24–48 hours
  • Temporary drooping of the eyelid (ptosis) or brow — rare, resolves as product metabolizes
  • Temporary asymmetry — may require touch-up treatment
  • Spread of toxin effect beyond injection site — rare
  • Allergic reaction — rare
  • Failure to achieve desired results
  • Results lasting shorter or longer than expected

Patient Acknowledgments

I understand that Botox is a temporary treatment and results will fade over time, requiring repeat treatments to maintain results.
I have disclosed all medications, supplements, and medical conditions to my provider and understand that withholding this information may increase my risk of complications.
I am not pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant in the near future.
I have not had Botox or any neurotoxin in the past 3 months unless disclosed to my provider.
I understand that no guarantee of results has been made and that individual outcomes vary based on anatomy, muscle strength, metabolism, and other factors.
I have been given the opportunity to ask questions and all of my questions have been answered to my satisfaction.
I have read and understood the pre-care and post-care instructions provided by this practice.
I consent to photographs being taken for medical record purposes. (Check if photos may also be used for marketing with patient permission — add separate photo release.)

Thank you for trusting us with your treatment today! 🤍 You're going to love your results. Please follow these post-care instructions carefully to protect your investment and get the best possible outcome. Text or call us anytime at [Phone Number] if you have questions.

1
The First 4 Hours — Most Important
🚫
Do NOT lie down for 4 hours. Remain upright — sitting or standing. Lying down too soon may cause the product to migrate to unintended areas.
🚫
Do NOT touch, rub, or massage the treated areas. This means no facials, no face washing with pressure, and no resting your face in your hands.
🏃
No strenuous exercise today. Increased blood flow and heart rate can spread the product and increase bruising. Light walking is fine. Skip the gym today.
🍷
No alcohol for 24 hours. Alcohol increases bruising risk. Give yourself the best chance at a clean result.
🌡️
Avoid heat today. No saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs, hot yoga, or direct sun exposure for 24 hours post-treatment.
2
Your Day-by-Day Timeline
Day 1

Treatment day — minimal activity

Stay upright, skip the gym, avoid alcohol and heat. You may have minor redness or small bumps at injection sites — this is completely normal and resolves within 1–2 hours.

Normal: tiny bumps, mild redness
Days 2–3

Early onset — you may start to notice it working

Some patients notice the first signs of the product working — a feeling of heaviness or mild tightness in the treated muscles. You may also have mild bruising. This is normal.

Possible: mild bruising, heaviness
Days 4–7

The product is settling in

You'll see more visible results during this window. The treated muscles are relaxing. Don't panic if results feel uneven — the full effect takes until day 14.

Be patient — not fully settled yet
Day 14

Full results — your follow-up appointment

This is when you see your complete results. We schedule all Botox follow-ups at the 2-week mark to assess your results and make any small adjustments at no additional charge.

Your complimentary follow-up appointment
Months 3–4

Time to rebook

Most patients begin to notice the product wearing off around months 3–4. We'll send you a rebook reminder. Consistent treatment schedules lead to longer-lasting results over time.

We'll remind you — no need to track it
3
What's Normal vs. What's Not

✓ Completely normal

  • Mild redness at injection sites
  • Small bumps that resolve in 1–2 hrs
  • Minor bruising lasting 3–5 days
  • Mild headache day of treatment
  • Feeling of heaviness or tightness
  • Slight asymmetry before day 14
  • Results appearing gradually over 7–14 days

✗ Contact us if you experience

  • Significant eyelid drooping
  • Difficulty swallowing or breathing
  • Spreading weakness beyond treated area
  • Signs of allergic reaction — hives, swelling
  • Vision changes
  • Bruising that worsens after day 3
  • Any concern that doesn't feel right

When in doubt, reach out. We would always rather you text us with a question than worry alone. Our number is [Phone Number]. We're here for you through the entire healing process — not just during your appointment.

When will I see my Botox results?▾
Botox begins working within 3–7 days and reaches its full effect at day 14. We include a complimentary 2-week follow-up with every Botox appointment so we can assess your results and make any fine adjustments.
Does Botox hurt?▾
Most patients describe Botox as a quick, small pinch — far less uncomfortable than expected. We use very fine needles and work efficiently. If you're anxious, let us know and we can apply numbing cream before your appointment.
How long does Botox last?▾
For most patients, Botox lasts 3–4 months. With consistent treatment over time, many patients find their results last longer as the treated muscles gradually soften.
Can I exercise after Botox?▾
Please skip strenuous exercise the day of your treatment. Light walking is fine. You can return to your normal workout routine the following day.
Can I wear makeup after Botox?▾
We recommend waiting at least 4 hours before applying makeup to the treated areas. When you do apply makeup, use a gentle touch — no rubbing or pressing on the injection sites.
What if my results look uneven?▾
Slight asymmetry before day 14 is completely normal — the product settles at different rates in different muscles. Always wait until day 14 before assessing your results. That's exactly why we schedule your complimentary follow-up at 2 weeks.
Will Botox make me look frozen?▾
Not when done correctly. We are a conservative practice. Our goal is results that make you look refreshed — not done. You will never leave our practice looking overdone.
When should I rebook?▾
We'll send you a rebook reminder at 10–12 weeks. Most patients book their next appointment at their 2-week follow-up so they always have a spot reserved. Consistent scheduling leads to longer-lasting results.
