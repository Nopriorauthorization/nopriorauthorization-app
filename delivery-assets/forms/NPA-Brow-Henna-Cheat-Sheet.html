MIX
Mix henna with warm distilled or purified water to a smooth paste — consistency of yogurt. Mix well — no lumps. Allow to bloom 1–2 minutes before application. Apply immediately — don't let sit.

📐 Brow Mapping — Techniques & Measurements
GOLDEN RATIO
3 points: Head (inner brow) aligns with outer nostril. Arch peak aligns with outer iris (looking straight). Tail aligns with outer corner of eye to outer nostril diagonal.
TOOLS
Brow mapping thread or ruler. White/nude pencil to mark. Measure both sides before removing any hair. Map first, shape second, tint third. Always.

🧹 Skin Prep Protocol
STEP 1
Double cleanse: Remove all makeup and skincare from brow area. Micellar water first, then oil-free cleanser. Residual product = poor color uptake and uneven staining.
STEP 2
Exfoliate: Gentle exfoliation with dry cotton pad or soft brush removes dead skin cells. Better skin prep = longer-lasting skin stain. Especially important for oily or build-up-prone clients.
STEP 3
Degrease: Wipe with alcohol-free toner or prep solution. Removes residual oil. This step dramatically improves henna skin stain longevity on oily skin types.
STEP 4
Barrier protect: Apply thin layer of barrier cream (Vaseline, barrier balm) just outside the brow area — not on brows themselves. Prevents color bleeding onto skin outside mapped area.
KEY
The difference between a 2-day and 2-week skin stain is almost always skin prep. Clients who moisturize heavily or use facial oils will have shorter-lasting stains regardless — educate at consultation.
✂️ Brow Shaping Methods
WAX
Hard or soft wax. Fast, precise. Best for clients with coarse, dense hair. Apply in direction of growth, remove against. Clean brow line and underneath. Wax before tinting — open follicles absorb color better.
THREAD
Twisted cotton thread. Precision hair removal. Best for defining clean lines. No product on skin. Great for sensitive skin (no chemicals). Slower than wax but very precise.
TWEEZE
Individual hair removal. Best for stray hairs and fine finishing after wax/thread. Never tweeze cold — warm area first. Pull in direction of growth. One hair at a time.
RAZOR/DERMAPLANING
Blade shaping — precise underside definition. Use for clients who can't tolerate wax. Also removes vellus hair = smoother application. Not recommended for acne-prone skin.
ORDER
Always: Map → Shape → Tint/Henna. Never tint before shaping — you'll remove the color you just applied. Never shape over freshly tinted skin — irritates sensitized skin.
🚫 Contraindications
ABS
Henna absolute CI: Known henna allergy. Active skin infection or open wounds in brow area. PPD allergy (for hybrid/traditional tints). Recent chemical peel or laser in brow area (<2 weeks).
ABS-TINT
Traditional tint absolute CI: PPD (para-phenylenediamine) allergy — must patch test all new clients 48hrs before service. PPD is in nearly all oxidative brow tints. Anaphylaxis risk.
REL
Relative: Accutane (within 6 months — extreme skin sensitivity). Psoriasis or eczema in brow area. Pregnancy (elective — client decision with consent). Recent Botox near brow (<2 weeks — avoid manipulating area).
WAXING CI
Waxing contraindications: Accutane. Active rosacea or eczema. Recent chemical peel or laser (<2 weeks). Sunburned skin. Blood thinners (bruising/bleeding risk). Botox — same area (<2 weeks).
PATCH
Patch test mandatory for all new tint/henna clients — 48 hours before service. Apply mixed product to inner arm or behind ear. Any redness, itching, swelling = do not proceed.
✅ Application Protocol — Henna
1
Prep skin — cleanse, exfoliate, degrease, barrier cream around brows.
2
Map and shape — measure, mark with pencil, wax/thread/tweeze to mapped shape.
3
Mix henna — warm water to yogurt consistency. Bloom 1–2 min.
4
Apply first layer — sparse areas and tail first. Apply with angled brush in hair stroke direction for defined look, or filled for blocked look.
5
Build layers — apply 2–3 coats, allowing each to slightly set before next. Builds depth and skin stain intensity.
6
Process — per skin/hair type timing chart. Do not cover (unlike lash lift — air exposure is fine for henna).
7
Remove — dry cotton pad first, then damp pad. Never water to remove — dry removal first preserves skin stain. Assess depth, apply additional layer if needed.
⚠️ Correction & Troubleshooting
TOO DARK
Over-saturated result: Apply oil (coconut or olive) to brow area immediately — oil lifts henna. Gently exfoliate. For tint: apply dandruff shampoo with circular motion — lifts oxidative color. Act fast.
TOO LIGHT
Under-processed / too light: Re-apply immediately with longer processing time. Ensure skin was properly prepped. For henna — second application same day is acceptable.
UNEVEN
Patchy/uneven color: Uneven skin prep or application. Re-apply to sparse areas only. Use angled brush for precision fill on thin areas.
STAIN OUTSIDE
Color bled outside brow: Remove immediately with oil-soaked cotton swab while still wet. Once dry — gentle exfoliation. Barrier cream prevents this — apply before every service.
REACTION
Allergic reaction: Remove product immediately. Cool compress. Antihistamine. If severe or spreading — refer to ED. Document and do not rebook for this service. Refer to dermatologist.
📋 Aftercare Instructions
24HR
No water, steam, or sweat on brow area. No makeup over brows. No touching or rubbing. Henna continues to develop and deepen for 24 hours post-removal.
ONGOING
Avoid oil-based skincare over brows — accelerates fading of skin stain. No exfoliating products (AHAs, BHAs, retinol) over brow area — removes skin stain faster.
SWIM
Chlorinated water and saltwater fade skin stain significantly. Apply thin layer of Vaseline over brows before swimming. Pat dry — don't rub after swimming.
REBOOK
Every 3–5 weeks for maintenance (tint refresh + wax cleanup). Henna skin stain lasts 2–4 weeks. Hair tint lasts 4–6 weeks. Standing monthly appointments = consistent revenue + client retention.
