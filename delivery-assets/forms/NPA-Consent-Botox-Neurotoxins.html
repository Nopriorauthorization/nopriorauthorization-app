About This Treatment
Botulinum toxin type A (Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau, or Daxxify) is an FDA-approved injectable medication that temporarily relaxes targeted muscles to soften dynamic wrinkles and achieve cosmetic or therapeutic results. Effects typically appear within 3–7 days, reach peak results at 14 days, and last approximately 3–4 months. Results vary by individual. I understand this is an elective cosmetic procedure and no guarantee of specific results has been made to me.
Risks, Side Effects & Potential Complications
I understand that the following risks and side effects are possible with neurotoxin injections. I have had the opportunity to ask questions and receive answers to my satisfaction.
Temporary bruising, redness, swelling at injection sites
Headache or flu-like symptoms (24–48 hours)
Asymmetry or uneven results
Eyelid or brow ptosis (drooping) — temporary
Unwanted spread to adjacent muscles
Results less effective or shorter than expected
Dry eye or excessive tearing (near orbital area)
Difficulty with facial expressions (temporary)
Allergic or hypersensitivity reaction (rare)
Antibody resistance with repeated treatments (rare)
Infection at injection site (rare)
Difficulty swallowing, speaking, or breathing (very rare)

Important: If you experience difficulty swallowing, speaking, or breathing after treatment, seek emergency medical care immediately. These symptoms may indicate spread of toxin effect beyond the injection site.
