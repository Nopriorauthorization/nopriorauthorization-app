About This Service
Eyelash extensions are synthetic fibers bonded to individual natural lashes using cyanoacrylate-based adhesive. They shed naturally with the lash growth cycle (every 60–90 days) and require fill appointments every 2–3 weeks to maintain fullness. Extension weight and length must never exceed what the natural lash can safely support.

Patch Test: A patch test is recommended for all new clients and required for anyone with allergy history, sensitive eyes, or prior eye procedures.   Offered: □   Accepted: □   Declined by client: □

Risks & Potential Complications
Eye irritation from adhesive fumes during application
Allergic reaction to cyanoacrylate — may be delayed 24–72 hours
Eyelid swelling, redness, or itching
Natural lash damage or traction alopecia from heavy extensions
Stickies — natural lashes bonded together
Eye infection if aftercare is not followed
Poor retention from oily skin, products, or humidity
Temporary or permanent natural lash loss from repeated damage
