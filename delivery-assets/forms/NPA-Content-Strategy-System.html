Why established spas go silent — and what actually fixes it

This is not a beginner problem

Your Content Audit — Fix What's Already Broken

For established spas: before you post one more thing, run this audit on what you've already built

Why this module exists — and why it matters for you specifically

Established spas have history. That history has patterns — gaps, imbalances, missed opportunities. Before you add more content, you need to understand what your current content is and isn't doing. This audit takes 30 minutes and tells you exactly what to fix first so that every new post you create lands better than everything that came before it.

Run this audit on your last 30 posts

What % are educational vs. promotional? If promotional is over 30% — you're selling too hard and your audience is tuning out.
Count and calculate
Are you showing your face? Scroll your last 30 posts. If your face appears fewer than 6 times — your audience doesn't know who you are yet. That's a booking problem.
Count face posts
Do all your posts have a CTA? "Link in bio," "DM me," "Book now" — if over half your posts have no CTA, you're generating awareness with no conversion path.
Check every caption
What's your average days between posts? More than 7 days = inconsistent in the algorithm's eyes. More than 14 days = essentially invisible.
Calculate the average
Which service gets the most posts? If one service dominates your feed, potential patients for your other services don't know you offer them.
Count by service
Do you have any before/after content? Before/afters are the #1 conversion driver for aesthetic accounts. Zero before/afters = zero social proof in the most powerful format.
Count B/A posts
Is your bio link a booking link? If your bio links to your homepage instead of a direct booking page — you're adding one step that costs you bookings every single day.
Check right now
What's your most saved post? Saves signal high-value content. Your most saved post tells you exactly what your audience finds most useful — and you should make 5 more like it.
Check analytics
What to do with your audit results

The three fixes that move the needle fastest

  • Change your bio link to a direct booking link today — this is a 30-second fix that immediately improves conversion from every future post
  • Post your face three times in the next two weeks — a Reel where you talk to camera, a photo of you in the room, a BTS moment. Nothing builds trust faster.
  • Add a CTA to every caption going forward — "Book via link in bio" or "DM me [service] to get started" — one line, at the bottom, every single time
