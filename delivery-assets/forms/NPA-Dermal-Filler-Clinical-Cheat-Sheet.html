💊 Hyaluronidase — Dissolving Protocol
PRODUCT
Hylenex (recombinant, preferred) or Vitrase (ovine). Skin test in allergy-prone patients. Both 150u/mL concentration.
IndicationDoseReassess

VO!
Vascular occlusion: Inject hyaluronidase immediately and aggressively. Massage vigorously. Warm compress. Aspirin 325mg. Vision changes = call 911 NOW.

🔧 Technique Reference
LINEAR
Linear threading: Advance needle fully, inject on withdrawal. NLFs, lip border, jawline.
SERIAL
Serial puncture: Multiple small boluses. Lips, tear trough, perioral. Precise placement.
BOLUS
Bolus: Single injection at target. Deep structural — cheeks, chin, jawline supraperiosteal.
CANNULA
Blunt cannula 22–27g: Single entry point. Fewer bruises. Lower vascular risk. NLFs, cheeks, tear trough, temples, marionettes preferred.

⚠️ Vascular Danger Zones
GLABELLA
Supratrochlear + supraorbital arteries. HIGHEST RISK — blindness. Small volumes, slow injection. Avoid deep bolus. Cannula preferred.
NOSE
Dorsal nasal + angular artery. Post-rhinoplasty = unpredictable anatomy. Extreme caution. Small volumes only. Aspiration each injection.
TEMPLE
Superficial temporal artery. Palpate before treating. Inject medial and deep. Cannula strongly preferred.
NLF
Facial artery — deep to superficial at NLF midpoint. Deep injections safer. Cannula reduces risk significantly.
LIPS
Superior/inferior labial arteries. Stay submucosal. Inject vermilion border — not central philtrum columns.
INFRAORB
Infraorbital foramen — infraorbital artery exits here. Stay lateral and superficial when treating mid-cheek.
🚨 Vascular Occlusion — EMERGENCY PROTOCOL
STOP
→
Hyase 150–300u NOW
→
Massage hard
→
Warm compress
→
ASA 325mg
SIGNS
Blanching, pain, livedo pattern, dusky/blue discoloration. Immediate action — do not wait to see if it resolves.
VISION
Vision changes = call 911 immediately. Retinal artery occlusion = irreversible blindness within minutes. No delay.
REPEAT
Repeat hyaluronidase q1hr PRN. Document everything. Follow up q24hr until fully resolved.
PREP
Always have hyaluronidase + ASA 325mg + warm compress IN the treatment room. Not down the hall.
⚠️ Complication Management
TYNDALL
Bluish tinge — HA too superficial (tear trough most common). Treat: 5–20u hyaluronidase precise placement. Prevent: inject deeper.
NODULE
Inflammatory nodule: Warm compress, massage, antihistamine. Granuloma: Hyaluronidase + intralesional steroid. Biofilm: Antibiotics + dissolve.
BRUISE
Arnica gel/oral. Cold compress immediate. Cannula reduces bruising 3:1 vs needle. Avoid NSAIDs/alcohol 48hr pre.
MIGRATION
Product moved from injection site. Dissolve and replace with appropriate G prime product for that area.
NECROSIS
End-stage VO. Aggressive hyaluronidase. Wound care. Plastic surgery referral. Thorough documentation for medico-legal.
✅ Pre-Treatment Checklist
CONSULT
3D assessment — front, oblique, profile. Prior filler history — when, what, where, how much. Previous complications.
PHOTO
Anterior + bilateral obliques + profile. Pre-existing asymmetry documented + patient acknowledged on consent.
HOLD
ASA/NSAIDs/fish oil 48hr. Alcohol 24hr. Dental work 2 wks pre AND post. Active cold sore — reschedule lip filler.
SCREEN
Autoimmune disease. Allergies (lidocaine, HA, albumin). Pregnancy. Active infection. Accutane within 6 months.
CONSENT
VO risk including blindness. Bruising, asymmetry, migration, nodules. Dissolving option (HA only). No guarantee of results.
🧠 High-Yield Clinical Pearls
★
Treat the cause, not the symptom. NLFs from volume loss — fill cheeks, not the fold.
★
Less is always more. Underfill, review at 2 weeks. Overfilling requires dissolving to fix.
★
Cannula for high-risk zones: Tear trough, NLFs, temples, glabella. Significantly reduces VO risk.
★
Match G prime to depth. High G prime = deep supraperiosteal. Low G prime = superficial lips.
★
Hyaluronidase in the room. Every single treatment. Not in a drawer down the hall.
★
Never assess before day 14. Swelling distorts results. Never add product before swelling resolves.
★
Sculptra rule of 1s: 1 vial per decade of age, 5–8mL dilution, 5-5-5 massage, 3 sessions minimum.
★
Document pre-existing asymmetry at consult. Photograph and have patient acknowledge on consent form.
