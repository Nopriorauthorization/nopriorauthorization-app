🧪 Compounded GLP-1 — Regulatory Status
STATUS
FDA declared semaglutide shortage resolved (2024) — compounded semaglutide 503B outsourcing facilities faced discontinuation orders. Status changes frequently — verify current FDA guidance before prescribing.
503A
503A compounding pharmacies (patient-specific): permitted for individualized patient prescriptions with documented clinical need. Physician oversight required.
503B
503B outsourcing: bulk compounding — regulatory scrutiny high in 2024–2025. Verify pharmacy is FDA-registered and compliant.
!
Never compound tirzepatide base — not on FDA shortage list. Tirzepatide salts (acetate, citrate) — legal gray area, avoid until clear guidance issued.
🚫 Contraindications
ABS
Absolute: Personal/family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia type 2 (MEN2). Hypersensitivity to product.
ABS
Pregnancy — discontinue at least 2 months before planned pregnancy. Not studied in pregnancy. Teratogenic risk unknown.
REL
History of pancreatitis — use with caution. Monitor amylase/lipase if symptoms arise. Contraindicated if active pancreatitis.
REL
Severe GI disease — gastroparesis, IBD, prior GI surgery. GLP-1 slows gastric emptying — may worsen existing conditions.
REL
Diabetic retinopathy — rapid glucose reduction can worsen retinopathy. Ophthalmology follow-up recommended.
REL
Renal impairment — use caution. Semaglutide not renally cleared but GI side effects can cause dehydration + AKI. Monitor.

😣 Side Effect Management
NAUSEA
Most common (40–50%). Peaks weeks 1–4 of each dose increase. Management: small frequent meals, avoid fatty/spicy food, ginger supplements, Zofran 4mg PRN, domperidone (off-label). Most resolves within 2–4 weeks.
VOMITING
Persistent vomiting — hold dose escalation. Consider antiemetic prescription. Ensure hydration. If severe — delay dose, reduce to prior tolerated dose.
DIARRHEA
Common especially tirzepatide. Loperamide PRN. Increase fiber slowly. Ensure adequate hydration. Usually self-limiting within 4–6 weeks.
CONSTIPATION
GLP-1 slows GI motility. Increase water + fiber. MiraLax PRN. Encourage movement. Common at higher doses.
FATIGUE
Common first 4–8 weeks. Ensure adequate caloric intake — minimum 1200 kcal. Prioritize protein. Usually self-resolving.
PANCREATITIS
Severe abdominal pain radiating to back, nausea/vomiting. HOLD medication. Check amylase/lipase. ER referral if suspected. Do not rechallenge if pancreatitis confirmed.
GALLBLADDER
Rapid weight loss increases cholelithiasis risk. Counsel patients. RUQ ultrasound if biliary symptoms develop.
🥩 Nutrition Protocol
PROTEIN
Priority #1: Minimum 1g protein per pound of goal body weight daily. GLP-1 causes muscle loss if protein inadequate. Lean meats, Greek yogurt, eggs, protein shakes.
CALORIES
GLP-1 reduces appetite significantly. Ensure minimum 1200 kcal/day (women) / 1500 kcal/day (men). Too-low calories = muscle wasting + nutrient deficiency.
HYDRATION
Minimum 64–80 oz water daily. Dehydration risk increases with nausea/vomiting/diarrhea. Electrolytes if significant GI losses.
AVOID
Fatty, fried, spicy foods worsen nausea. Alcohol + GLP-1 = increased hypoglycemia + worsened GI effects.
EXERCISE
Resistance training essential to preserve muscle mass during weight loss. Minimum 2x/week strength training. Cardio adjunctive.

💉 Injection Technique
SITES
Rotate weekly: Abdomen (2 inches from navel), anterior thigh, upper arm (outer). Document rotation. Never inject same site consecutively.
TECHNIQUE
Pinch 1 inch of skin. 90° angle (45° if thin). Insert full needle length. Inject slowly over 5–10 seconds. Hold 5–10 seconds after. Remove. Do not rub.
TIMING
Same day each week. Time of day does not matter — patient preference. Can take with or without food. With food if nausea is significant.
STORAGE
Unopened: Refrigerate 36–46°F. Do not freeze. Once opened: refrigerate or room temp up to 56 days (semaglutide), 30 days (tirzepatide). Protect from light.
MISSED
Missed dose: If within 5 days — inject as soon as remembered. If more than 5 days — skip and resume next scheduled dose. Never double dose.
🛑 When to Discontinue
STOP
Confirmed pancreatitis. MTC diagnosed. Severe allergic reaction. Pregnancy confirmed — discontinue immediately, refer to OB.
STOP
Persistent vomiting/inability to eat for 48+ hours — hold until resolved. Reassess dose reduction vs discontinuation.
CONSIDER
No weight loss after 16 weeks at therapeutic dose — reassess adherence, nutrition, dose. Consider alternative medication or evaluation for secondary causes.
REBOUND
Weight regain is expected on discontinuation — counsel every patient before starting. This is chronic disease management, not a temporary treatment.
🧠 High-Yield Clinical Pearls
★
Slow titration = patient retention. The #1 reason patients stop is GI intolerance from rushing the dose.
★
Set expectations at month 1. Month 1 is about tolerating the drug, not losing weight. Patients who know this stay on the program.
★
Protein is non-negotiable. Muscle loss is real. Patients who don't hit protein targets will lose muscle not just fat.
★
Tirzepatide is more effective but also more expensive and more GI side effects at initiation. Start semaglutide if cost is concern.
★
Verify compounding pharmacy regulatory status every quarter. The landscape changes — protect your license and your patients.
★
MTC black box warning is based on rodent studies. Human risk unclear but contraindication stands. Always screen family history.
★
Oral medications timing matters. Thyroid medication, oral contraceptives — take 1 hour before GLP-1 injection day to avoid delayed absorption.
