Key Reminders — Hormone Therapy
★ Always treat to SYMPTOMS + LABS together — never labs alone. A patient with optimal numbers who still feels terrible needs reassessment.
★ Mammogram current before initiating estrogen therapy. PSA baseline before initiating testosterone in men.
★ Progesterone must be used in women with an intact uterus receiving estrogen — unopposed estrogen increases endometrial cancer risk.
★ Pellet therapy is IRREVERSIBLE once inserted — cannot be removed. Side effects must be managed until pellet dissolves (3–6 months).
★ Hematocrit must be monitored in all men on TRT. Phlebotomy or dose reduction if >54%.
