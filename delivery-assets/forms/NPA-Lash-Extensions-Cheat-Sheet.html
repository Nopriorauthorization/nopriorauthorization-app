RULE
Extension should never be heavier than the NL can support. Extension diameter ≤ NL diameter. Length: no more than 2–3mm longer than NL for health. Prioritize NL integrity always.
🗺️ Eye Mapping Guide
CAT EYE
Shortest inner corner → longest at outer ¾ → taper short at very end. Creates lifted, foxy look. Length differential 2–4mm from inner to peak.
DOLL EYE
Longest lashes directly above pupil. Taper shorter to both corners. Opens and rounds the eye. Best for almond and small eyes.
SQUIRREL
Peak length at 60–70% mark (not outer corner). Creates natural lifted effect. Most universally flattering mapping style.
NATURAL
Mirror natural NL growth pattern. Gradual increase then taper. Minimal length variation (1–2mm range). Perfect for clients wanting subtle enhancement.
HOODED
For hooded lids: use longer outer corner, avoid over-length at inner corner. CC/D curl to lift. Avoid L curl (lies flat under hood).
CLOSE-SET
Avoid length and weight at inner corner. Emphasize outer ¾. Creates visual width. Cat-eye or squirrel mapping best.

STORE
Unopened: cool, dark, airtight. Opened: upright, airtight bag with silica packets. Use within 4–6 weeks of opening. Never refrigerate opened glue. Shake well before use.
HUMIDITY
Use hygrometer in your room. Below 40% = too dry, poor bonding. Above 65% = too humid, shock polymerization. Optimal: 45–60%. Use humidifier or dehumidifier to control.
✅ Application Protocol
PREP
Remove all eye makeup. Cleanse with oil-free lash cleanser. Prime with lash primer (removes residual oil, improves retention). Place under-eye pads. Tape down lower lashes if needed.
ISOLATE
Perfect isolation is everything. One NL at a time. Use two tweezers — isolating and applying. Extension adheres to isolated NL only. Stickies = poor isolation = damage and shedding.
APPLY
Dip extension base 2–3mm into adhesive droplet. Wipe off excess on edge of glue stone. Apply to NL base — 0.5–1mm from skin. Hold 1–2 seconds. Release cleanly.
CURE
Nanomist or nano-sprayer at end of application — accelerates cure, reduces fume exposure, improves retention. Hold 30cm away, 2–3 short bursts. Fan dry before opening eyes.
COMPLETE
Check for stickies before client opens eyes. Brush through with spoolie. Remove tape gently. Advise: no water 24–48 hours. No oil-based products near eyes. No rubbing.

🚫 Contraindications
ABS
Absolute: Known cyanoacrylate allergy. Active eye infection (pink eye, stye, blepharitis flare). Active chemotherapy. Trichotillomania (compulsive pulling — extensions worsen). Recent eye surgery (<6 months).
ABS
Severe alopecia affecting lashes. If fewer than 50% of NL remain — extensions will further stress remaining lashes. Refer to dermatology first.
REL
Relative: Sensitive eyes/contact lens wearers (remove lenses before service). Thyroid disease (lash loss — manage expectations). Trichotillomania (mild) — with consent and counseling. Pregnancy — sensitivity increased.
REL
Blepharitis (mild, controlled): Clients must be symptom-free and maintain daily cleansing routine. Extensions increase risk of flare. Get medical clearance. Educate on daily lash washing requirement.
PATCH
Offer patch test 48hrs before service for new clients or anyone with allergy history. Apply small amount of adhesive behind ear or on inner arm. Assess for reaction before proceeding.
⚠️ Complication Management
ALLERGY
Allergic reaction to adhesive (cyanoacrylate): Eyelid swelling, redness, itching — can worsen over 24–72hrs. Remove extensions immediately with adhesive remover. Cool compress. Antihistamine (Benadryl). If severe — refer to MD. Switch to sensitive/latex-free adhesive for future.
DAMAGE
NL damage/traction alopecia: Thin, short, bent NL from over-heavy extensions or poor isolation. Remove extensions. NL rest period 4–8 weeks. Castor oil nightly. Lighter set when returning.
STICKIES
Stickies (lashes bonded together): Separate with two tweezers immediately — gently twist to break bond. If cure is complete — use adhesive remover on stuck point. Prevent with slower adhesive + better isolation technique.
RETENTION
Poor retention causes: Oily skin/NL, residual product, wrong humidity, wrong adhesive, improper isolation, client touching/rubbing, oil-based products, excessive heat/steam. Address root cause.
📋 Aftercare — Client Instructions
24HR
No water, steam, sweat, or humidity near eyes for 24 hours. No saunas, hot yoga, swimming pools. No crying (when possible 😊). Adhesive needs full cure time.
ALWAYS
Wash lashes daily with oil-free lash shampoo. Use only oil-free products near eyes. Sleep on a silk pillowcase. Brush with spoolie daily. No mascara (especially waterproof).
NEVER
No oil-based makeup removers, moisturizers, or sunscreens near eyes. No rubbing. No pulling or picking at extensions. No waterproof mascara — removal damages extensions and NL.
REBOOK
Every 2–3 weeks for fills. Extensions shed with NL growth cycle (every 60–90 days). Book fills before 40% loss for best results and NL health. Offer standing appointment system.

🧠 High-Yield Lash Pearls
★
Isolation is the most important skill. Everything else — curl, length, mapping — is secondary to perfect isolation. Stickies cause damage. Damage loses clients.
★
Replace adhesive every 20–30 min. Adhesive skins over rapidly. Fresh drop = consistent dry time and bond strength. Never work from a skin-over drop.
★
Control your environment. Hygrometer in your room. Know your humidity. Change adhesive for the season — summer vs. winter adhesive speeds differ significantly.
★
NL health first, always. If NL are too thin, damaged, or short — educate the client. A lash rest period now = a long-term client later. Short-term fills on damaged NL = long-term client loss.
★
Sell lash shampoo at every appointment. Clients who wash daily retain 30–40% longer. Better retention = happier client = better reviews = more referrals. It's your best retail item.
★
Standing fill appointments = consistent income + client retention. Offer booking at checkout: "Should I book your fill now before my schedule fills?" 80% say yes.
