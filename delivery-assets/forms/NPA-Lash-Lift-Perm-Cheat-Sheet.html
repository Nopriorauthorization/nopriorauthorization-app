🔬 Chemistry — How It Works
STEP 1
Lifting/Perming Solution: Thioglycolate or cysteamine breaks disulfide bonds in lash cortex. Lash becomes moldable around the shield shape. This is the chemical processing step.
STEP 2
Setting/Fixing Solution: Oxidizing agent (hydrogen peroxide) reforms disulfide bonds in new lifted position. Locks the curl permanently (until lash naturally sheds).
STEP 3
Nourishing/Keratin Step: Optional but recommended. Replenishes protein and moisture lost during chemical processing. Improves result quality and lash health. Apply after removal of shield.
CYSTEAMINE
Cysteamine vs. Thioglycolate: Cysteamine = gentler, less odor, growing in popularity. Thioglycolate = traditional, stronger, odor. Both work well. Cysteamine preferred for fine or damaged lashes.

TIMING
Tinting done after lifting is complete and shield/rod is removed. Apply tint, process, remove. Do not tint before — solution residue affects tint uptake. Patch test required — especially for new clients.
FDA
Lash tints are NOT FDA approved for use around eyes. Use professional-grade lash-specific tints. Informed consent recommended. Do not use hair dye near eyes — different formulation, higher risk.
✨ Keratin Lash Lift — Protocol Notes
WHAT
Keratin lash lift = standard lift chemistry + keratin protein infusion. Result: lifted lashes with added shine, smoothness, and health. Duration same as standard lift (6–8 weeks).
BRANDS
Popular systems: Elleebana, Yumi Lashes, Sugarlash PRO, Belmacil, Iconsign. Each has proprietary timing and protocol. Always follow brand-specific instructions. Timing is NOT interchangeable between brands.
BENEFIT
Keratin step adds protein to cortex — improves lash health with each service vs. standard lift which can be drying over time. Better for clients who lift frequently (every 6 weeks).
UPSELL
Upgrade from standard lift to keratin lift: +$15–25 retail. Cost difference is minimal. High perceived value. Script: "I recommend upgrading to our keratin lift — it conditions your lashes while lifting them so they actually get healthier over time."

CLEANSE
Oil-free cleanser. Remove all makeup. Dry completely before applying shield glue — moisture impairs adhesion.
SHIELD
Apply shield glue to lid. Place shield. Press to ensure adhesion. Lashes must lay completely flat on shield surface before applying solution.
ADHERE
Apply lash-to-shield adhesive. Comb lashes up and onto shield with Y-comb or lash tool. Every lash must be individually adhered — no clumping.
PROCESS
Apply Step 1 to lash body (not roots). Wrap with cling film to retain heat. Set timer per lash type. Check visually at minimum time.
FIX
Remove Step 1 with damp cotton. Apply Step 2 immediately. Process per timing chart. Remove. Apply nourishing oil or keratin step.
🚫 Contraindications
ABS
Absolute: Known thioglycolate or cysteamine allergy. Active eye infection. Very short lashes (under 4mm — nothing to adhere to shield). Recent Accutane (within 6 months). Active chemotherapy.
ABS
Lash extensions currently on: Cannot lift over extensions. Remove extensions, wait minimum 2 weeks before lash lift. Adhesive residue interferes with chemistry.
REL
Relative: Pregnancy (elective chemical service — client decision). Very fine/damaged lashes — risk of over-processing. Thyroid disease (lash texture changes). Previous chemical damage.
REL
Blepharitis: Active flare = no service. Controlled blepharitis with daily cleansing routine = service with consent. Solutions can exacerbate inflammation.
PATCH
Patch test for new clients or first-time chemical service. Apply small amount of Step 1 solution to inner arm or behind ear 48hrs before. Check for redness, burning, swelling.
⚠️ Corrections & Complications
OVER-PROCESSED
Frizzy, crimped, or over-curled result: Cannot reverse — damage to disulfide bonds is permanent until lash sheds. Apply nourishing oil or keratin treatment to smooth appearance. Rebook in 6–8 weeks.
UNDER-LIFT
Not enough lift / dropped quickly: Resistant lashes — next service increase Step 1 time. Can re-lift after 6 weeks. Do not re-process same day — risk of over-processing compromised lashes.
UNEVEN
Uneven curl: Lashes not fully adhered to shield. Some fell during processing. Improve adhesion technique next service. Cannot correct current result — rebook at 6 weeks.
IRRITATION
Eye irritation/chemical burn: Flush immediately with cool water. Remove shield. Apply cool compress. Do not neutralize with Step 2 — rinse only. Document. Refer to ophthalmologist if persistent.
📋 Aftercare Instructions
24HR
No water, steam, sweat, or eye rubbing for 24 hours. No mascara for 24 hours. Disulfide bonds continue to set for 24 hours post-service.
ONGOING
Sleep on back or side (not face-down). Avoid oil-based eye products at lash line. Use conditioning lash serum to maintain health. Brush daily with spoolie.
REBOOK
Every 6–8 weeks. Lift grows out with NL — cannot redo before 6 weeks without risking over-processing already-lifted lashes. Standing appointments dramatically improve retention rates.
SELL
Retail at checkout: lash conditioning serum, nourishing lash oil, spoolie brush. Package as "Lash Lift Maintenance Kit" — $25–40 retail. Clients who use conditioning products rebook at higher rates.

🔄 Brow Lamination Add-On
SAME
Brow lamination uses same chemistry as lash lift. Same Step 1 (lift) and Step 2 (fix) solutions. Process simultaneously to save time — do brows during lash lift Step 1 processing time.
TIMING
Brow Step 1: 6–10 min (fine/sparse brows 6 min, coarse/thick brows 10 min). Brow Step 2: 5–8 min. Brush into desired shape after Step 1, before applying Step 2.
SHAPE
Brush brow hairs up and slightly outward (fluffy/soap brow look) OR brush flat and straight for sleek result. Client preference. Hold in place with clean spoolie during Step 2.
PRICE
Add brow lamination to lash lift appointment: +$45–65. Combined service 90 minutes. Total appointment: $150–200+. Most popular combo service in the industry right now.
