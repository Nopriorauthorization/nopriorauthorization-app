Danger Zones — Lips
⚠️ Superior labial artery: runs variably within lip body — position changes between individuals. Aspirate before each deposit when using needles.
⚠️ Avoid deep injection at midline philtrum — superior labial artery runs here in most patients.
⚠️ Oral commissure / modiolus: multiple muscle convergence point — inject conservatively, avoid deep bolus.
⚠️ Blanching during injection = STOP immediately. Massage vigorously. Have hyaluronidase ready in treatment room at all times.
Lip Flip vs Lip Filler — When to Use Which
Lip Flip (Botox 4–6 units)
No volume added — eversion only
Shows more of the lip you already have
Lasts 6–8 weeks
Cannot replace lost volume
Best: gummy smile + subtle enhancement
Cost: $50–80 typically
Onset: 3–5 days
Lip Filler (HA 0.5–1.5mL)
Adds actual volume and definition
Can reshape, define, and volumize
Lasts 6–12+ months
Reversible with hyaluronidase
Best: volume loss, thin lips, definition
Cost: $500–900 typically
Swelling: peaks 24–72 hrs, resolves 2 wks
Clinical Pearls
14-day ruleAlways assess at 14 days — do not add more filler before swelling fully resolves
Pre-treatmentNo blood thinners, alcohol, or NSAIDs 48–72 hrs before. Manage HSV with prophylactic antivirals if history present
Cannula entryEntry point lateral to oral commissure using needle, then switch to 25–27g blunt cannula for body
Natural movementTest lip movement throughout injection — patient should still be able to purse, smile, and speak naturally
Cold compressApply immediately post-treatment to reduce swelling. Arnica gel helps bruising. Avoid heat 24 hrs
