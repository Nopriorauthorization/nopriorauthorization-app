Post-Microneedling Protocol
Hours 0–24Apply only provider-recommended products (hyaluronic acid serum, growth factor). No active ingredients. No makeup. Gentle cleanser only.
Hours 24–72Skin will appear red, feel warm, may be mildly swollen. Normal. Continue gentle skincare. Apply SPF 50+ when going outside.
Days 3–5Peeling and flaking begins. Do not pick or peel. Hyaluronic acid + moisturizer. Some patients have no visible peeling — also normal.
Days 7–14Skin normalizes. May reintroduce retinoid and active ingredients gradually. SPF 50+ daily for minimum 2 weeks post-treatment.
AvoidSun exposure, saunas, hot showers, exercise (48–72 hrs), swimming, makeup (24 hrs), active ingredients (5–7 days depending on depth)
GHK-Cu / growth factorsApply immediately post-treatment — micro-channels enhance penetration dramatically. Excellent for boosting collagen stimulation results.
Contraindications — Microneedling
Absolute Contraindications
Active acne breakout in treatment area
Active skin infection (bacterial, viral, fungal)
Accutane within 6–12 months
Active rosacea flare in treatment area
Keloid or hypertrophic scar history
Blood coagulation disorders
Pacemaker or implanted metal near area (RF devices)
Pregnancy
Relative Contraindications (Proceed with Caution)
Diabetes — impaired healing, higher infection risk
History of HSV — pre-treat with antivirals
Immunosuppression — slower healing
Recent sunburn in treatment area
Eczema, psoriasis in treatment area
On blood thinners — increased bruising
Very dark Fitzpatrick (V–VI) — PIH risk. Conservative settings mandatory
Wound healing disorders
Microneedling + Combination Treatments
✓ GHK-Cu copper peptide: apply immediately post-needling — channels dramatically improve delivery. Enhances collagen stimulation.
✓ PRP (platelet-rich plasma): apply to skin during needling or post-procedure. Significantly accelerates healing and results.
✓ Vitamin C serum: apply post-needling (L-ascorbic acid delivers deeper via channels). Enhanced brightening and collagen support.
⚠️ Retinoids: do NOT apply post-needling — too irritating on compromised skin. Resume after full healing (7–14 days).
⚠️ AHAs/BHAs: do NOT apply post-needling for same reason. Channels make penetration unpredictable and excessive.
