📐 Olympia Ordering & Logistics
ORDER
Olympia Compounding Pharmacy: olympiapharmacy.com. Provider account required. DEA registration needed for controlled substance orders. Your rep can set up standing orders for common kits.
SHIP
Cold-chain shipping for temperature-sensitive products. Inspect packages on arrival — if cold packs are fully melted and products are warm, do not use. Contact Olympia rep for replacement.
STORAGE
Refrigerate 2–8°C upon arrival. B12 and B-complex may be room temp (check individual product). NAD+ refrigerate — use within 30 days of reconstitution. ALA — refrigerate + protect from light.
STANDING
Set up standing orders with Olympia for your most-used kits. Reduces order time, ensures consistent supply, locks in pricing. Ask your rep about volume discounts for monthly orders.
CATALOG
Olympia catalog changes periodically — products added and removed. Verify availability before promising specific formulas to patients. Download current formulary from provider portal quarterly.
EXPIRY
Check expiration dates on every vial at receipt. Never use expired compounded medications. BUD (beyond use date) may be shorter than printed expiration — follow whichever is sooner.

📋 Olympia Patient-Specific vs. Office Use
503A
Olympia 503A (patient-specific): Requires individual patient Rx with patient name, DOB, prescriber info. Cannot be kept as office stock. Most peptides and hormones fall here.
OFFICE
Office-use products: Some Olympia products can be ordered for office use (not patient-specific) — typically vitamins, minerals, standard IV additives. Confirm with your rep which products qualify.
STANDING RX
For patient-specific products (B12, MIC, glutathione), some providers use standing orders — physician-signed protocol covering a category of patients. Check your state's standing order rules for compounded medications.
COMPLIANCE
Keep all Olympia invoices, Rx copies, lot numbers, and BUD dates. Required for state board inspection. Document the Olympia lot number for every compounded product administered.
⚠️ Special Precautions — Olympia Products
ALA
Alpha Lipoic Acid: Extremely light-sensitive — degraded by light within minutes. Always use amber IV bag or wrap clear bag in aluminum foil. Administer immediately after preparation. Discard unused portion.
NAD+
NAD+: Rate-dependent side effects — chest tightness, nausea, cramping if run too fast. Never push as IV bolus. Minimum 2 hours for 500mg. Slow the rate — don't stop the infusion.
PLAQUEX
Plaquex (PPC): Must be run slowly — 2–3 hours. Some patients experience flushing, nausea, headache. Do not combine with other additives. Dedicated bag and dedicated IV line.
GLUTA
Glutathione: Never add to IV bag — oxidizes immediately. Always IV push after bag complete. Some patients experience sulfur taste/smell — normal. Rare: bronchospasm in asthmatic patients.
HDVC
High-dose Vitamin C (>10g): G6PD deficiency test required before administration. Risk of hemolytic anemia in G6PD-deficient patients is life-threatening. No exceptions to this screening rule.

🧠 High-Yield Clinical + Business Pearls
★
ALA must be light-protected immediately. Don't mix and set down — wrap in foil the moment it's prepared. Degraded ALA is inactive ALA.
★
G6PD is non-negotiable before high-dose Vit C. One hemolytic crisis destroys your practice. Test once, file it, never forget it.
★
NAD+ side effects = too fast. Slow the rate. Do not stop the infusion. These always resolve with rate reduction.
★
Glutathione add-on is your highest-margin upsell. $8 cost, $40–65 retail, 3 minutes of work. Offer it to every IV patient.
★
Name your drips experientially. "Glow Drip" sells. "Glutathione + B-complex infusion" doesn't. Patients buy results and experiences, not ingredients.
★
