Why two pre-appointment texts?

The 48-hour text handles logistics and prep — it reduces no-shows and prepares the patient clinically. The morning-of text builds emotional excitement and reduces anxiety. Patients who arrive calm and prepared have better treatment experiences and better outcomes. Both texts together reduce no-shows by approximately 30–40% compared to no outreach.

Send within 30 minutes of patient leaving

This text is the most important one in the entire sequence. Patients who receive post-care instructions immediately after the appointment follow them. Patients who don't receive them Google "is it normal that my Botox isn't working on day 3" — and end up anxious and calling. Send this within 30 minutes of them leaving.

The week-one window — why this matters

Days 5–10 are when patients are most likely to panic, call, and leave a negative review before their results have even fully developed. Sending the day-7 text pre-empts that window. A patient who gets this text doesn't panic — they wait. And when they see their results at day 14, they're delighted instead of relieved.

At the 2-week appointment — what to do

  • Show the patient their before/after photos side by side — let them react before you say anything
  • Ask: "How do you feel about them?" — their answer tells you everything
  • Assess for asymmetry, underdosing, or any areas they want adjusted
  • Book the next appointment before they leave — this is the rebooking moment
  • Ask for a Google review — in person, right now, while they're happy

The review timing rule

Ask for the review in person first — look them in the eye and say "if you're happy with your experience, would you be willing to leave us a Google review?" Then send the link by text within an hour. In-person ask + immediate link = highest conversion rate. Waiting more than 24 hours drops conversion by more than half.

The rebooking math

  • Patients who prebook at the 2-week visit return at 85%+ rate
  • Patients who receive the day-75 text return at 60–70% rate
  • Patients who receive no outreach return at 30–40% rate
  • The difference between those rates is the difference between a thriving practice and one that constantly chases new clients
How to use this kit

The complete sequence at a glance

  • 48 hours before: Confirmation + prep instructions
  • Morning of: Day-of excitement text
  • Same day (post-appointment): Post-care recap + expectations
  • Day 3: Proactive check-in
  • Day 7: Week-one reassurance
  • Day 14: 2-week follow-up + book complimentary assessment
  • Day 14 + 48hrs: Follow-up if no response
  • After 2-week visit: Review request (same day)
  • Day 75: First rebook touchpoint
  • Day 90: Rebook follow-up if no response
  • Seasonal: Occasion-based rebooking

How to set this up in your practice

  • Load all texts into your EMR or CRM system with automated triggers by date
  • Popular tools: Vagaro, Jane App, Mindbody, Spruce Health, or even a simple Google Sheet + manual send
  • Personalize every text with the patient's first name — never send a mass blast
  • Save each message as a template in your phone or CRM so it takes 15 seconds to send
  • Assign one team member as the "communication owner" — one person sends all texts, every time

Adapt for filler — same sequence, minor edits

Replace "Botox" with "filler" and adjust the timeline: filler check-in at day 14 for swelling assessment, with the understanding that final results settle at 4 weeks. The rebooking window for filler is 9–12 months rather than 3–4 months. All other texts remain identical.

HIPAA reminder: Text messaging with patients requires their written consent to receive SMS communications. Include an SMS consent field in your intake form. Keep consent records in the patient chart. Standard text is not HIPAA-secure — use a HIPAA-compliant messaging platform (Spruce, Klara, or your EMR's messaging feature) for clinical discussions.
