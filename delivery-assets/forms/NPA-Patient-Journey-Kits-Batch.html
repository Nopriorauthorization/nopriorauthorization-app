Pre-Care key content

  • Eat a meal before — critical for NAD+
  • Drink 16oz water before arriving
  • Wear loose comfortable clothing
  • Disclose all medications and allergies
  • History of kidney disease or CHF — must disclose
  • No IV if fever or active infection

Post-Care key content

  • Mild bruising at insertion site — normal
  • Continue hydrating the rest of the day
  • NAD+ — flu-like feeling is normal and temporary
  • Energy benefits may take 24hrs to feel
  • How often to come back per goal
  • When to call — extravasation signs

IV therapy patients who aren't prepped properly are your most anxious patients — they didn't eat, they're dehydrated, and they don't know what the dripping sensation means. We send a pre-care guide 24 hours before every IV appointment that ends with "eat a real meal, drink a full water bottle, and wear a shirt you can roll the sleeve up on." Sounds simple but it eliminates 80% of the anxiety calls. We have patient journey kits for IV therapy if anyone wants them. — Danielle, Hello Gorgeous Med Spa

Microneedling Patient Journey Kit

RF microneedling and traditional. The "no actives" rule and the redness timeline are everything. Patients need to know day 3 will look worse before it looks better.

$57
Queued

Pre-Care key content

  • Stop retinoids 5 days before
  • Stop actives — AHAs, BHAs, vitamin C — 3 days before
  • No sun exposure 1 week prior
  • No Accutane within 6 months
  • No active acne lesions in treatment area
  • Arrive with clean skin — no makeup

Post-Care key content

  • Redness timeline — day 1 red, day 3 sandpaper, day 5 peeling
  • No actives for 5 days post
  • Only gentle cleanser and barrier cream
  • No sun for 2 weeks — SPF is non-negotiable
  • No makeup for 24hrs minimum
  • Series of 3–6 for optimal collagen remodeling
FB Group comment for this kit

Microneedling patients who weren't warned about day 3 are your panicked callers. Day 3 looks like sandpaper — that's the skin doing exactly what it's supposed to. We send a day-by-day redness and texture guide with every microneedling appointment: Day 1 red, Day 2 tight, Day 3 rough texture (this is normal!), Day 5 starting to peel, Day 7 glowing. When patients know the roadmap they stop panicking and start trusting. We have full pre and post care kits for microneedling. — Danielle, Hello Gorgeous Med Spa

Chemical Peel Patient Journey Kit

NPA-Chemical-Peel-Patient-Journey-Kit

Superficial, medium, and deep peels. The peeling timeline days 3–7 is the most important content. Sun avoidance is the #1 post-care rule.

$57
Queued

Pre-Care key content

  • Stop retinoids 5–7 days prior
  • No waxing or hair removal 1 week prior
  • No sun exposure 2 weeks prior
  • Prep skin with gentle cleanser only week of
  • Disclose all medications — especially Accutane
  • Plan social downtime for days 3–7

Post-Care key content

  • Peeling timeline — starts day 2–3, peaks day 4–5
  • Never pick or pull peeling skin
  • Only provider-approved gentle moisturizer
  • SPF 30+ every single day — no exceptions
  • No active ingredients for 2 weeks
  • Results visible at day 10–14
FB Group comment for this kit

Chemical peel patients who pick at their peeling skin are your hyperpigmentation callbacks. The instruction sheet we give says it three different ways: do not pick, do not peel, do not touch. But also — we always tell patients to plan their social calendar around the peel. "Day 4 and 5 you will look like a snake shedding its skin. Plan accordingly." Honesty upfront prevents callbacks. We have pre and post care kits for chemical peels if helpful. — Danielle, Hello Gorgeous Med Spa
