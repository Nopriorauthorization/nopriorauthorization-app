Insertion Technique — Key Steps
SiteUpper outer buttock — gluteal fat pad. Lateral to gluteal crease, medial to iliac crest. Mark site with pen.
PrepBetadine and alcohol prep. Sterile field. Lidocaine 1–2% with epinephrine — infiltrate skin and tract to fascia.
Incision2–3mm incision through skin only with a #11 scalpel. Do not go too deep.
Trocar insertionInsert trocar through incision into the subcutaneous fat layer parallel to fascia. Advance toward iliac crest.
Pellet deliveryLoad pellet into trocar. Advance stylet to deliver pellet. Withdraw trocar while holding stylet in place.
ClosureSteri-strips or Dermabond to close incision. No sutures needed. Apply pressure 60 seconds.
Post-careNo soaking (baths/pools/hot tubs) for 5–7 days. No strenuous lower body exercise 3–5 days. Bruising normal.

Complications — Recognition & Management
ComplicationSignsManagement
InfectionRedness, warmth, purulent drainage, feverAntibiotics (Keflex 500mg QID x 7–10 days). Rarely requires pellet removal
ExtrusionPellet visible under skin, migrating toward incisionApply pressure dressing. Rarely extruded fully — monitor closely
Fibrosis / scarringHard nodule at insertion site on subsequent insertionsRotate insertion sites. Massage area post-insertion to prevent
Bruising / hematomaSignificant swelling or bruising at siteIce, pressure, NSAIDs if no contraindication. Resolves 1–2 weeks
High testosterone (women)Acne, oily skin, clitoral changes, hair growthCannot remove pellet — manage side effects pharmacologically until dissolves
High estradiol (men)Gynecomastia, water retention, mood changesAnastrozole 0.5mg 2x/week. Lower T dose at next pellet
