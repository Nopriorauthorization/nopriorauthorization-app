🩹 BPC-157 — Body Protection Compound
WHAT
15-amino acid peptide derived from gastric juice protein. Promotes healing — tendon, ligament, muscle, gut, nerve. Strong safety profile in animal studies. No human clinical trials completed.

FDA placed BPC-157 on Bulk Substances list (2024) — cannot be compounded at 503A/503B. Verify current regulatory status before prescribing. Landscape changes rapidly.
✨ GHK-Cu — Copper Peptide
WHAT
Naturally occurring copper-binding peptide. Promotes wound healing, collagen synthesis, anti-inflammatory. Strong evidence for topical skin rejuvenation. Injectable use — research ongoing.
RouteDoseFrequencyUse Case

Topical GHK-Cu has strong evidence: increases collagen density, reduces fine lines, promotes wound healing. Excellent adjunct to microneedling/RF treatments.
💜 PT-141 (Bremelanotide) — Sexual Health
WHAT
Melanocortin receptor agonist. Acts centrally on sexual arousal pathways — hypothalamus. FDA approved (Vyleesi) for HSDD in premenopausal women. Off-label use in men for erectile function.
PopulationDoseRoute/TimingNotes
Women (HSDD)1.75 mgSubQ, 45 min before activityFDA-approved Vyleesi dose
Men (ED)0.5–2.0 mgSubQ, 1–2 hrs beforeOff-label. Start low — 0.5 mg.
Compounded0.5–1.75 mgSubQVerify pharmacy + current regs
SE
Side effects: Nausea (most common — 40%), flushing, transient BP increase. Contraindicated with high CV risk. Avoid in uncontrolled HTN, cardiovascular disease.
LIMIT
Max 1 dose per 24 hours. No more than 1 dose per 8 days (Vyleesi labeling). Rotate SubQ sites. Nausea peaks 30–60 min post-dose.
🌙 Epithalon (Epitalon)
WHAT
Tetrapeptide — Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly. Telomere-lengthening properties in research. Pineal gland support. Circadian rhythm optimization. Strong anti-aging interest. No FDA approval.
DOSE
Protocol: 5–10 mg/day SubQ or IM for 10–20 day course. Repeat 2x/year. Some protocols: 100 mcg/kg. Research-based dosing — highly variable in literature.
TIMING
Evening/before bed preferred — supports melatonin/circadian rhythm. Some use morning. No consensus on optimal timing.
STATUS
Not FDA approved. No human clinical trials. Research basis only — animal and in vitro studies. Verify regulatory status before prescribing. Gray area for compounding.
🧬 Thymosin Alpha-1 & Beta-4
TA-1
Thymosin Alpha-1: Immune modulator. 1.6 mg SubQ 2x/week. Used for chronic infections, immune deficiency, post-COVID immune support, cancer adjunct. FDA approved (Zadaxin) outside US.
TB-4
Thymosin Beta-4: Tissue repair, anti-inflammatory, cardiac protection. 1–5 mg SubQ 2–3x/week. Strong healing properties. Athletic recovery, chronic injury. Not FDA approved.
TB-500
TB-500: Synthetic TB-4 fragment. Same mechanism, more available. 2.5–5 mg SubQ 2x/week. Used for tendon/muscle/ligament healing. Popular in athletic populations.
STATUS
TB-4 and TB-500 — compounding availability variable by state and current FDA guidance. Verify before prescribing. Patient consent for off-label/research use essential.
🧪 Reconstitution Guide — All Peptides
DILUENT
Bacteriostatic water (BAC water) — preferred for multi-use vials. Sterile water — single-use only. Never use tap water or saline unless specified.
Vial SizeBAC WaterConcentration Result
2 mg peptide2.0 mL1 mg/mL = 1000 mcg/mL
5 mg peptide2.5 mL2 mg/mL = 2000 mcg/mL
10 mg peptide5.0 mL2 mg/mL = 2000 mcg/mL
TECHNIQUE
Insert needle at angle, let BAC water run down the glass wall — do not inject directly onto powder. Roll gently — never shake. Allow to dissolve fully (1–2 minutes).
DOSE CALC
Example: 5mg vial + 2.5mL BAC = 2mg/mL. Want 200mcg dose = 0.1mL (10 units on U-100 insulin syringe).
STORAGE
Unmixed lyophilized: Refrigerate or room temp. Protected from light. Reconstituted: Refrigerate 2–8°C. Use within 30 days. Mark date on vial.
DISCARD
Discard if: cloudy, particulate, discolored, or more than 30 days since reconstitution.
💉 Injection Technique — Self-Injection Protocol
NEEDLE
U-100 insulin syringe: 28–31g, 5/16" or 1/2" length. SubQ injection — pinch skin, 45° angle. IM — 23–25g, 1–1.5" length, perpendicular.
SITES
Rotate every injection: Abdomen (2" from navel), anterior thigh, upper arm (lateral). Avoid same site within 7 days. Document rotation.
TECHNIQUE
Clean with alcohol swab — let dry 30 seconds. Pinch 1" skin. Insert at 45°. Inject slowly. Remove. Light pressure (no rubbing). Recap and dispose properly.
TIMING
GH secretagogues: 30 min before bed on empty stomach (no food 2hrs prior). Fasting enhances GH pulse amplitude significantly.
MISSED
Missed dose — skip and resume next scheduled dose. Do not double up. GH peptides work cumulatively — consistency matters more than individual doses.

🧠 High-Yield Clinical Pearls
★
GH secretagogues on empty stomach. Food — especially carbs — blunts GH pulse. 2hrs fasting before injection. Most important compliance factor.
★
Ipamorelin is cleanest GHRP — no cortisol, no prolactin, no hunger side effect. First-line choice for most patients over older GHRPs (GHRP-2, GHRP-6).
★
Verify regulatory status quarterly. FDA landscape on peptides is one of the fastest-changing areas in clinical practice. Your license depends on prescribing only what is currently permissible.
★
Label reconstituted vials. Date, concentration, patient initials. 30-day rule — discard after 30 days post-reconstitution regardless of remaining volume.
★
Results are cumulative. GH secretagogues work over months — not weeks. Patients need 3-month minimum commitment. Consistency > dose.
★
IGF-1 monitoring is non-negotiable for GH peptides. Supratherapeutic IGF-1 is associated with cancer risk. Stay within upper-normal reference range.
★
Informed consent for off-label use. Document that patient understands these are compounded medications with limited clinical trial data. Use 503A/503B registered pharmacies only.
