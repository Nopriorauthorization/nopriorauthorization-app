💊 Compounding Pharmacy Categories
503A
503A Traditional Compounding Pharmacy: Patient-specific prescriptions only. Cannot compound in bulk for office use. Most compounded hormones, peptides, and topicals fall here. Requires individual patient Rx.
503B
503B Outsourcing Facility: FDA-registered. Can produce in larger quantities. Can supply without patient-specific Rx (for office use/stock). Higher oversight — cGMP standards. More expensive but more scalable.
VERIFY
Always verify pharmacy is state-licensed and FDA-registered. Check NABP (National Association of Boards of Pharmacy) database. Use only PCAB-accredited pharmacies when possible.
NEVER
Never use compounded medications from unregistered sources. Patient safety + provider liability. If it's not from a licensed pharmacy — it's not safe to prescribe.

📜 Prescription Writing — Required Elements
REQUIRED
Every valid prescription must include: Patient full name + DOB. Date written. Drug name, strength, quantity. Directions (sig). Prescriber name, credentials, address, DEA# (if controlled). Signature.
CONTROLLED
Controlled substance Rx extras: DEA number mandatory. Some states: tamper-resistant paper required. Electronic prescribing for controlled substances (EPCS) required in many states. Check your state board.
E-PRESCRIBE
EPCS (electronic prescribing controlled substances) now mandatory in many states. Requires DEA-compliant software + two-factor authentication. Check your state's deadline if not yet compliant.
TELEHEALTH
Telehealth prescribing of controlled substances: Ryan Haight Act + DEA special registration for telehealth. In-person evaluation typically required for initial controlled substance Rx. Check current DEA telehealth rules.
🩺 Anaphylaxis Protocol — Step by Step
1
EPINEPHRINE FIRST. 0.3mg IM into anterolateral thigh. No delay — epi before antihistamines. EpiPen if auto-injector available. Repeat in 5–15 min if no improvement.
2
Call 911 immediately after administering epi. Anaphylaxis can biphasic recur 4–8 hours later. All anaphylaxis requires ER evaluation.
3
Position: Supine with legs elevated (if no respiratory distress). If respiratory distress — allow patient to sit up. Never stand a hypotensive patient.
4
Secondary medications: Diphenhydramine 25–50mg IV/IM. H2 blocker (Pepcid 20mg IV). Corticosteroid (Solu-Medrol 125mg IV) if available — adjuncts only, not substitutes for epi.
SIGNS
Anaphylaxis triad: Urticaria/angioedema + hypotension + bronchospasm. Two or more systems involved after allergen exposure = anaphylaxis. Act immediately — don't wait for all three.

📐 Controlled Substance Storage Requirements
STORAGE
Schedule II–V: Locked, substantially constructed cabinet or safe. Cannot be left unlocked or accessible to non-authorized personnel. Separate from non-controlled medications when possible.
LOGS
Dispensing logs required: Patient name, date dispensed, quantity, lot number, prescriber signature. Testosterone logs must be maintained and available for DEA inspection.
INVENTORY
Biennial inventory required by DEA for all registered facilities. Record all controlled substances on hand. Document losses or thefts immediately on DEA Form 106.
THEFT
Any theft or significant loss of controlled substances must be reported to DEA and local law enforcement within 1 business day. DEA Form 106. Non-reporting is a federal violation.
🧠 High-Yield Prescribing Pearls
★
Valacyclovir before lip filler in any patient with HSV history. 500mg BID starting 3 days before and continuing 3 days after. Prevent outbreak triggered by trauma.
★
Apraclonidine (Iopidine) is the only intervention for Botox-induced lid ptosis. Have it in your dispensary. 1–2 drops TID to affected eye until resolved.
★
Toradol max 5 days. Renal toxicity + GI bleed risk increases with duration. Never combine with aspirin or other NSAIDs.
★
Phenergan is NEVER IV push. Severe tissue necrosis — cases of limb amputation reported. PO or deep IM only. No exceptions.
★
Aminoglycosides potentiate Botox. Screen for gentamicin, tobramycin, neomycin use before injecting neurotoxins. Enhanced effect can cause adverse spread.
★
Isotretinoin: wait 6–12 months after course completion before any ablative procedures, microneedling, or dermabrasion. Impaired wound healing during and after treatment.
★
DEA registration required to prescribe testosterone and other Schedule III substances. Verify your NPI, DEA#, and state CDS registration are all current before prescribing.
