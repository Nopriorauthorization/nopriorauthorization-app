Managing Side Effects by Gender
Men — Common Side Effects & Management
High E2 (>40 pg/mL): anastrozole 0.5mg 2x/wk or reduce T dose
High hematocrit (>52%): therapeutic phlebotomy, reduce dose, increase hydration
Testicular atrophy: HCG 500IU 2x/wk preserves testicular function and fertility
Acne: benzoyl peroxide wash, dietary changes. Rarely dose adjustment
Mood changes early in treatment: usually normalizes. If persistent — check E2 and levels
Hair thinning: genetic predisposition. Finasteride if appropriate after discussion
Women — Common Side Effects & Management
Acne: reduce dose. Benzoyl peroxide. Spironolactone rarely needed if using appropriate T doses
Facial hair growth: reduce dose. This is most common reason women stop TRT
Voice deepening: PERMANENT at high doses — start low, monitor closely. Irreversible
Clitoral growth: dose-dependent. Reduce if bothersome — partially reversible
Hair thinning (androgenic alopecia): reduce dose. Rogaine if needed
Irritability / mood changes: often resolves. Check levels — may be too high or too low
