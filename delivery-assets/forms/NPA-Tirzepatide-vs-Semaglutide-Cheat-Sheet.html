Mechanism of Action
Semaglutide (Ozempic / Wegovy)
GLP-1 receptor agonist ONLY
Reduces appetite via hypothalamic GLP-1 receptors
Slows gastric emptying
Stimulates insulin release (glucose-dependent)
Suppresses glucagon
Single pathway — one mechanism
Tirzepatide (Mounjaro / Zepbound)
DUAL GIP + GLP-1 receptor agonist
GIP activates additional fat metabolism pathways
More powerful appetite suppression via dual CNS action
Enhanced insulin sensitivity through both pathways
Suppresses glucagon more completely
Two pathways = additive metabolic effect

Key Clinical Reminders
★ Both medications treat a CHRONIC biological condition — weight regain is expected when discontinued. Counsel patients before starting.
★ Protein intake is critical for both — minimum 0.8–1g per pound of ideal body weight daily to prevent muscle loss.
★ Neither medication is approved in: MTC/MEN2 personal or family history, active pancreatitis, pregnancy.
★ Monitor for mood changes — GLP-1 receptors are in the brain. Rare reports of depression and suicidal ideation.
★ Drug interactions: both delay gastric emptying — oral medications (especially thyroid, contraceptives) may have altered absorption.
