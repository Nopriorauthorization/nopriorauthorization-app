Category 01
Injectable Products
Danielle's note
"Buy neurotoxins and fillers only through authorized distributor accounts with the manufacturer — Allergan, Galderma, Merz, Evolus. Never buy injectables from a third party, gray market, or anyone who doesn't require verification of your medical credentials. The gray market is real and products are being counterfeited. It is not worth it."

Category 02
Compounding Pharmacies
Danielle's note
"Only work with 503A pharmacies that are licensed in your state and PCAB-accredited if possible. The compounding pharmacy landscape changes constantly — verify licensing and regulatory status every quarter. Your rep can tell you what they can currently compound. Ask specifically and document the answer."

Verify before you order

Always confirm: (1) Current state license — check your state pharmacy board website. (2) PCAB accreditation if available. (3) BAA availability for HIPAA compliance. (4) Current regulatory status for any specific compound you intend to prescribe — especially GLP-1 and BPC-157 which have faced FDA scrutiny.

Category 06
Equipment & Technology Vendors
Danielle's note — on equipment buying
"Equipment is where you can really go wrong. I have over $500K invested in equipment at Hello Gorgeous. Here is what I've learned: (1) Always request a demo before you buy. (2) Check the service and warranty terms before you sign. (3) Talk to other providers who own that specific device — not just the reps. (4) Consider financing carefully — a device that doesn't produce revenue is a liability, not an asset."
