📊 Hard Wax vs. Soft Wax — When to Use Each

RULE
Hard wax for everything bikini and above. Soft wax for large body surfaces. Hybrid approach: soft wax legs and arms, hard wax face, underarms, and bikini/Brazilian. Best of both worlds.

TRIM
Hair longer than 1/2" should be trimmed before waxing — not shaved. Trimming scissors or clippers to 1/4–1/2". Too-long hair wraps around spatula and breaks instead of removing from follicle.

💆 Brazilian Waxing — Area Map & Technique
SECTIONS
Work in sections: Front triangle → left side → right side → inner labia (if requested) → perianal strip. Never jump around — systematic approach reduces missed hair and client discomfort.
POSITIONS
Client positions: Frog-leg for inner labia and perianal. Standard position for front triangle. Ask client to hold skin taut when needed — reduces trauma and improves removal.
TECHNIQUE
Apply against growth direction. Remove parallel to skin, against growth. Never pull upward — lifts skin. Keep hand pressed flat immediately after removal to reduce trauma response.

💉 Pre-Wax Prep by Area
CLEANSE
Cleanse all areas with pre-wax cleanser before applying wax. Removes oil, sweat, product buildup. Essential for adhesion and reducing bacterial transfer.
POWDER
Apply pre-wax powder (talc or cornstarch) to absorb residual moisture and oil. Creates dry surface for better hard wax adhesion. Skip powder with soft wax — impairs strip adhesion.
OIL
Pre-wax oil optional with hard wax — creates thin barrier that protects skin while wax grips hair. Use sparingly — too much impairs adhesion. Not recommended with soft/strip wax.
BRAZILIAN
Client should shower day of appointment. Clean, dry skin only. No lotion or oil applied to area. Trim if hair longer than 1/2". Avoid during menstruation (optional — more sensitive, client's choice with provider comfort).

🚫 Contraindications
ABS
Absolute: Accutane (isotretinoin) — current use or within 6–12 months. Sunburned skin. Active skin infection, open wounds, or active herpes outbreak on area. Blood thinners (Coumadin, warfarin) — high bruising/bleeding risk.
ABS
Recent procedures: Chemical peel, laser, microneedling, or dermaplaning (<2 weeks). Radiation therapy to area. Varicose veins (legs) — do not wax directly over. Recent Botox (<2 weeks — same area).
REL
Relative: Diabetes (impaired healing — use caution). Active eczema/psoriasis in area — avoid affected skin. Rosacea on face — hard wax only, gentle technique. Pregnancy — increased sensitivity, generally safe.
MEDS
Medications increasing sensitivity: Retinoids (Retin-A, tretinoin — face waxing contraindicated), antibiotics (photosensitivity), blood thinners, steroids (thins skin). Always screen medications at intake.
⚠️ Complications & Management
LIFTING
Skin lifting/tearing: Skin removed with wax — common on face with retinoid use. Apply cold compress immediately. Barrier cream (Vaseline) to protect. Do not apply further products. Document. No re-waxing same area. Refer if wound develops.
BURN
Wax burn: Cool running water for 10+ minutes. Cool compress. Aloe vera gel. Do not pop blisters. Refer to MD if significant. Document and review wax temperature protocol.
INGROWN
Ingrown hairs: Most common on bikini area and legs. Management: gentle exfoliation 3x/week starting 72hrs post-wax. Tend Skin or AHA solution (glycolic). Release with sterile needle if visible. Prevent with standing exfoliation routine.
FOLLICULITIS
Folliculitis: Infected hair follicles — red bumps, pustules. Warm compress. Tea tree oil topically. Oral antibiotics if widespread. Prevent with proper post-wax antiseptic application and client hygiene education.
BREAKOUT
Post-wax breakout: Common on face. Apply witch hazel or salicylic acid immediately post-service. No makeup for 4–6 hours. No touching face. Advise client of this possibility before service.
✅ Aftercare Instructions
4HR
No heat — no hot baths, saunas, hot tubs, sun exposure. No tight clothing over waxed areas. No exercise (sweat + open follicles = bacteria + breakout). No makeup on waxed facial areas.
24HR
No self-tanners, perfumed products, or exfoliants. No swimming (chlorine + open follicles). No sexual activity after Brazilian for 24 hours (friction + bacteria risk).
72HR
Begin exfoliation at 72 hours — 3x/week going forward. Use a gentle physical or chemical exfoliant (glycolic, lactic, salicylic). This is the most important ingrown prevention step.
RETAIL
Sell at checkout: ingrown prevention solution (Tend Skin, PFB Vanish), exfoliating mitt, soothing aloe gel. "Waxing Aftercare Kit" bundled = $25–40. Clients who use aftercare products rebook consistently.
📐 Application Technique — Body Areas
DIRECTION
Apply in direction of hair growth. Remove against direction of growth, parallel to the skin surface (not upward). Removing upward lifts skin. Taut skin = clean removal = less trauma.
SECTIONS
Work in small sections — never apply wax to a larger area than you can remove in one motion. Large sections = uneven removal, more pain, more missed hair.
TAUT
Hold skin taut with non-dominant hand immediately before and during removal. This is the single most important technique for clean removal and reduced discomfort. Never skip.
UNDERARM
Underarm hair grows in 3 directions. Work in sections: apply/remove downward, then upward, then across. May need 3 separate applications. Hard wax only — sensitive area.
LEGS
Soft wax: apply thin layer in direction of growth (downward on legs). Apply strip, smooth firmly, remove against growth in one swift flat motion. Work top to bottom in horizontal sections.
🔄 Ingrown Hair Prevention System
WHY
Ingrown hairs occur when hair curls back or grows sideways into skin instead of up through follicle. Most common: coarse, curly hair, tight clothing over area, lack of exfoliation.
PREVENT
The 3-step client protocol: (1) Exfoliate 3x/week with mitt or AHA. (2) Moisturize daily. (3) Wear loose clothing for 24hrs post-wax. This eliminates 80% of ingrown issues.
TREAT
For existing ingrowns: Warm compress to soften. Sterile lancet to release trapped hair (if visible at surface). Tend Skin or glycolic solution daily. Never dig — refer if deep or infected.
RETAIL
Ingrown prevention is your best retail opportunity. Every Brazilian client needs it. Script: "The #1 thing that prevents ingrowns is consistent exfoliation starting 72 hours after your service — let me show you what we carry."
