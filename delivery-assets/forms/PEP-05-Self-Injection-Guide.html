Self-Injection Training Guide
Subcutaneous peptide & hormone injections
This guide is a reference tool. Please complete hands-on training with your provider before self-administering injections.
Supplies You Will Need
Prescribed medication vial
Insulin syringe (1mL, 29–31 gauge)
Alcohol swabs
Sharps disposal container
Clean flat surface
Before Every Injection
Wash hands for 20 seconds with soap and water
Confirm correct vial, dose, and expiration date
Inspect vial — solution should be clear and colorless
Allow refrigerated medication to reach room temperature (10–15 min)
Gather all supplies before you begin
Drawing Your Dose
1Wipe the top of the vial with an alcohol swab. Let dry.
2Draw air into the syringe equal to your prescribed dose.
3Insert the needle into the vial through the rubber stopper.
4Push the air into the vial (creates pressure for easier drawing).
5Invert the vial and syringe together.
6Draw the prescribed dose. Check for air bubbles — tap and push out.
7Remove needle from vial. Do not recap.
Performing the Injection
1Clean injection site with alcohol swab. Let dry completely (10–15 sec).
2Pinch a fold of skin with your non-dominant hand — about 1–2 inches.
3Hold syringe like a pencil in your dominant hand.
4Insert needle at 45–90 degree angle with a smooth, quick motion.
5Release the pinch once the needle is in.
6Slowly push the plunger all the way down with steady pressure.
7Withdraw the needle at the same angle. Apply gentle pressure — do not rub.
8Dispose of needle and syringe in sharps container immediately.
