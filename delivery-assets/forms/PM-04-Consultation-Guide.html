Preparation Guide
For New & Prospective Patients
We're so glad you're considering Hello Gorgeous for your aesthetic and wellness journey. This guide will help you feel confident and prepared for your first visit.
What Happens During a Consultation
  1. Warm welcome and intake form review
  2. One-on-one discussion of your aesthetic goals
  3. Comprehensive skin or area assessment
  4. Review of your medical history and current skincare routine
  5. Personalized treatment recommendations from your provider
  6. Before photos with your consent for progress tracking
  7. Treatment timeline and expected results discussion
  8. Pricing, payment options, and package review
  9. Time to answer all of your questions — no question is too small
  10. Typical duration: approximately 30–45 minutes
Questions to Ask Your Provider
  • What treatment do you recommend for my concern?
  • How many sessions will I need for optimal results?
  • What results can I realistically expect?
  • What are the risks and potential side effects?
  • What is the recovery time and downtime?
  • How long do results last before maintenance is needed?
  • What is the total cost of my recommended treatment plan?
  • Are there financing options or packages available?
  • What should I do before and after treatment to prepare?
  • When can I schedule my first treatment?
Treatment Options Overview
Injectables
Neurotoxins relax muscles to smooth lines and wrinkles. Dermal fillers restore lost volume and enhance facial contours. Results are visible quickly with minimal downtime.
Ideal for: fine lines, volume loss, lip enhancement, facial contouring
Skin Treatments
Medical-grade facials, chemical peels, microneedling, and LED therapy address texture, tone, acne scarring, and overall skin health. Results build with each session.
Ideal for: dull skin, uneven texture, hyperpigmentation, acne scars
Body Contouring
Non-invasive treatments to reduce stubborn fat, tighten skin, and smooth cellulite. Advanced technology delivers visible results without surgery or extended downtime.
Ideal for: stubborn fat, loose skin, cellulite, body shaping
Wellness Services
IV hydration, weight management programs, peptide therapy, and hormone optimization support your health from the inside out for lasting beauty and vitality.
Ideal for: energy, metabolism, weight loss, anti-aging, overall wellness
Setting Realistic Expectations
  • Some results are immediate (fillers, neurotoxins), while others develop over weeks (microneedling, Sculptra, chemical peels)
  • Multiple sessions may be needed for optimal, lasting results
  • Maintenance treatments help preserve and extend your results over time
  • Individual results vary based on age, skin type, lifestyle, and genetics
  • Your provider will create a customized plan tailored to your unique needs and goals
