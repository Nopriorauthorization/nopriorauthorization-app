What to Expect:
Your GLP-1 Journey
A month-by-month guide to your first 12 weeks
Starting GLP-1 therapy is a big step — and we want you to feel prepared, not surprised. This guide walks you through what's normal and how to set yourself up for success.
Before You Start

You'll complete intake paperwork, lab work, and a consultation. Your provider will confirm you're a good candidate and review your health history. Your starting dose will be lower than your maintenance dose — this minimizes side effects while your body adjusts.

Weeks 1–4 — Adjustment

What you'll likely feel:

Nausea — especially after injections and large meals
Reduced appetite — the medication working
Possible fatigue as your body adjusts
Constipation or loose stools (varies)

What helps:

Smaller portions — stomach capacity has changed
Eat slowly and stop when satisfied
Avoid greasy, fried, or very spicy foods early on
Stay hydrated — at least 64 oz daily
Ginger tea, small crackers, or cold foods for nausea
Expected weight loss month 1: 2–6 lbs typical
Weeks 5–8 — Finding Rhythm

What you'll feel:

Nausea usually lessens significantly
More consistent, predictable hunger
Improved energy as body adapts
A clearer sense of "satisfied" vs. "stuffed"

What to focus on:

Protein first at every meal — aim 25–30g per meal
Add movement if you haven't — even 20-minute walks
Avoid "slider foods" — choose nutrient-dense options
Expected weight loss month 2: 4–8 lbs additional
Weeks 9–12 — Building Momentum

What you'll notice:

Clothes fitting differently
Reduced cravings for high-sugar foods
Natural portion control
Improved energy and possibly better lab values
