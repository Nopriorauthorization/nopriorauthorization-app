1. Appointment Scheduling

Appointments may be scheduled by calling our office at 630-636-6193 or through our online booking portal. All appointments require confirmation; unconfirmed appointments may be released to other patients. We will send reminders via your preferred contact method, but it is ultimately your responsibility to keep track of your scheduled appointments.

2. Cancellation & No-Show Policy

We value your time and ours. We require a minimum of 48 hours' advance notice for all cancellations and reschedules.

PolicyFee
Late cancellation (less than 48 hours' notice)$50.00
No-show (missed appointment without notice)$75.00
Repeated no-shows (3 or more within 12 months)May result in discharge from the practice

Fees are charged to the card on file. Emergencies will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis at the discretion of management.

3. Late Arrival Policy

If you arrive more than 15 minutes past your scheduled appointment time, we may need to reschedule your visit to avoid impacting other patients. If your provider is able to see you, please understand that your appointment may be shortened and cannot be extended beyond the originally allotted time. Shortened appointments will be billed at the full rate.

4. Payment Policy

Payment is due in full at the time of service. We accept the following payment methods:

Cash

Important: All services provided at Hello Gorgeous Med Spa are cosmetic and elective in nature. We do not bill insurance. The patient is responsible for the full cost of all services, products, and treatments.
5. Minors Policy

Patients under the age of 18 must have a parent or legal guardian present at all appointments, including consultations. The parent or guardian must provide written consent for any and all treatments and must remain on the premises for the duration of the visit.

6. Communication Preferences

We may contact you regarding appointment reminders, follow-up care, and important health information via phone call, text message, or email based on the preferences you indicate on your Communication Consent Form. We will never share your contact information with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Fragrance-Free Environment

For the comfort and safety of all patients and staff, we maintain a fragrance-free environment. We kindly ask that you refrain from wearing perfume, cologne, or heavily scented products when visiting our office. Thank you for your consideration.
