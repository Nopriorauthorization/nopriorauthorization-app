Notice of Privacy Practices
Patient Summary · HIPAA Compliance
This is a plain-language summary of how Hello Gorgeous Med Spa uses and protects your personal health information under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). A complete Notice of Privacy Practices is available upon request.
How We Use Your Information

Your protected health information (PHI) may be used and disclosed for the following purposes:

PurposeWhat This Means
TreatmentWe use your health information to provide, coordinate, and manage your care. This includes sharing information with other providers involved in your treatment, such as referring physicians or specialists.
PaymentWe may use your information to process payments, prepare billing statements, and manage your account. If you use third-party financing (CareCredit, Cherry), limited information may be shared to process your application.
Healthcare OperationsWe use your information for quality improvement, staff training, compliance audits, and the day-to-day management of our practice to ensure we deliver safe, effective care.
Your Rights

Under HIPAA, you have the following rights regarding your health information:

RightDescription
Access Your RecordsYou may request to see and obtain a copy of your medical records and billing information. We may charge a reasonable fee for copies.
Request CorrectionsIf you believe information in your record is incorrect or incomplete, you may request an amendment. We may deny the request under certain circumstances but will provide a written explanation.
Request RestrictionsYou may ask us to limit how we use or share your information for treatment, payment, or operations. We are not required to agree to all requests but will carefully consider each one.
Choose How We Contact YouYou may request that we communicate with you in a specific way or at a specific location — for example, by email only or at a particular phone number.
Receive an AccountingYou may request a list of certain disclosures we have made of your health information for purposes other than treatment, payment, or operations.
File a ComplaintIf you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with our office or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. You will not be penalized for filing a complaint.
Our Responsibilities

Hello Gorgeous Med Spa is required by law to:

• Maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information
• Notify you promptly if a breach occurs that may have compromised your information
• Follow the terms of this notice and our full Notice of Privacy Practices
• Obtain your written authorization before using your information for marketing or selling your data
• Not use or disclose more information than necessary to accomplish the intended purpose
When We May Share Without Your Permission

In limited circumstances, we may be permitted or required to disclose your information without your authorization:

As required by federal, state, or local law
For public health activities and safety reporting
To prevent a serious and imminent threat to health or safety
For health oversight, audits, or investigations
In response to a court order or lawful subpoena
To coroners, funeral directors, or organ donation organizations
