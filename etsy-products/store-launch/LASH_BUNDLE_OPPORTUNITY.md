# Lash Bundle Opportunity

This is a non-medical expansion lane with strong Etsy demand.

It fits the same storefront mechanics as the med spa business:

- one niche buyer
- one strong bundle promise
- many reusable forms and marketing assets
- repeatable listing image structure
- high-perceived value with editable Canva files
- premium photo-led branding direction

## Product Concept

### Working Product Name

`Lash Business Templates Bundle`

### Core Buyer

- lash artists
- lash techs
- estheticians
- salon suite owners
- beauty service providers building a premium lash brand

### Why This Niche Works

- large Etsy search volume
- less clinical compliance friction than medical content
- forms and salon systems create high practical value
- buyers often want both operations templates and marketing templates together
- easy to repurpose into mini packs later

## Draft Listing Promise

`Lash business templates bundle for lash artists and estheticians. Includes Canva client forms, consent and intake templates, aftercare guides, plus Instagram posts, stories, and marketing content to attract clients and grow your lash brand.`

## Digital Product Notes

- editable in Canva
- works on desktop, tablet, and phone, though desktop is best
- instant download
- no physical shipping
- digital purchases are non-refundable
- personal use only, no resale
- Canva free or Pro element availability may change over time

## Visual Direction

Even though this is a non-medical beauty lane, it should still feel premium.

- use high-end beauty photography
- avoid cartoon people or illustration-led branding
- keep the bundle polished, feminine, modern, and luxury-forward

## Store Fit

This product can either:

1. live inside the same `No Prior Authorization` system as a beauty-business expansion line, or
2. become the start of a beauty-service sub-brand if you want to separate clinical and non-clinical storefront lanes later

## Suggested SKU Path
