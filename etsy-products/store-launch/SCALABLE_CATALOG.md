# Scalable Catalog

This is the growth engine for `No Prior Authorization`.

The goal is not to hand-build hundreds of unrelated products. The goal is to turn one clinical service line into multiple Etsy SKUs using the same visual system, listing system, and content-block structure.

## Core Idea

Think in two dimensions:

- rows = service lines
- columns = pack types

That means one topic can become many listings without inventing a new store every time.

## How One Service Becomes 6-10 Listings

1. `IV Therapy Social Media Bundle`
2. `IV Therapy Myths vs Facts Pack`
3. `IV Therapy Promo Pack`
4. `IV Therapy Story Templates`
5. `IV Therapy Caption Pack`
6. `IV New Patient Offer Pack`
7. `IV Membership Promo Pack`
8. `IV Therapy Seasonal Campaign Pack`

You do not need eight different design systems.
You need one IV content bank and a few reusable layout families.

## Bundle Architecture

- `Complete Med Spa Bundle` = IV + Weight Loss
- `Complete Injector Bundle` = Botox + Filler
- `Complete Clinical Bundle` = IV + Weight Loss + Injectables

Rule:

- entry listings point up to cores
- cores point up to bundles
- bundles state the savings versus buying separate

## Scaling Logic

You scale by reusing:

- one thumbnail system
- one 7-image listing system
- one content bank per service line
- one pricing ladder
- one trust message

You create a new listing when:

- the deliverable set is meaningfully different
- the buyer job is different
- the price tier is different
- the SEO intent is different enough to justify its own slot

You do not create a new listing when:

- it is only a typo fix
- it is the same product with barely changed screenshots
- it confuses the price ladder

## Store Growth Target

The realistic goal is:

- first 8 listings to establish the shop
- 15 listings to look substantial
- 25 listings to look serious
- 50 listings to look like a real storefront machine

You do not need 300+ listings to win in your niche.
You need a system that can keep expanding without breaking.
