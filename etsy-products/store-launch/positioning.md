# Store Positioning

## Core Buyer

`No Prior Authorization` is built for licensed med spa and wellness providers who need content that sounds clinically credible and sells consultations without sounding generic.

Primary buyers:

- Nurse practitioners and FNP-BCs running IV therapy programs
- Med spa owners offering IV hydration, GLP-1, injectables, or wellness services
- Aesthetic injectors expanding into wellness offers
- Mobile IV providers
- Weight loss clinics needing provider-led content
- Smaller practices without an in-house marketing team

## Store Promise

Provider-written captions and Canva templates for med spas that want to:

- save time
- look premium
- educate responsibly
- post consistently
- convert followers into booked consults

## What Makes This Store Different

- Built by real FNP-BCs, not generic social media marketers
- Grounded in real IV therapy and GLP-1 program workflows
- Compliance-aware by default, with no baked-in miracle claims
- Designed for free Canva and instant use
- Written with a direct provider-to-provider voice

## Trust Pillars

- Danielle Kent, FNP-BC and Ryan Kent, FNP-BC are named creators
- Backed by Hello Gorgeous Med Spa in Oswego, Illinois
- Includes compliance-oriented guides and disclaimer placeholders where appropriate
- Uses clinically literate language instead of vague wellness fluff
- Digital delivery is clear: instant download, no physical shipment, no resale

## Pricing Stance

The shop should be positioned as premium but practical.

- Entry products should reduce friction without cheapening the brand
- Core kits should reflect subject-matter depth and done-for-you volume
- Bundles should create an obvious value jump and raise average order value

Current anchors:

- IV Therapy bundle: `$47`
- Weight Loss bundle: `$57`
- Complete Med Spa bundle: `$67`

## Brand Voice

Write like a provider who is helping another provider move faster.

Voice rules:

- clear
- clinically informed
- direct
- anti-fluff
- supportive
- compliance-conscious

Avoid:

- hype-heavy promises
- vague wellness cliches
- guaranteed outcomes
- corporate agency language
- diet-culture framing

## Final Positioning Statement

`No Prior Authorization` is the Etsy shop for med spas, nurse practitioners, and wellness providers who want social media content that sounds like it came from clinic, not a copy farm. Every bundle is built by board-certified FNP-BCs and packaged to help providers post with authority, stay visually polished, and market responsibly.
