## FAQ

### What do I get after purchase?

You receive instant digital downloads that include captions, Canva access links, instructions, and compliance-related guidance based on the product.

### Do I need Canva Pro?

No. The templates are designed to work with free Canva.

### Are these templates compliance-safe?

They are compliance-aware and written to avoid sloppy claims, but each buyer is responsible for final review based on their own state board, scope, and practice rules.

### Who creates the content?

Danielle Kent, FNP-BC and Ryan Kent, FNP-BC from Hello Gorgeous Med Spa.

### Can I resell or share these files?

No. These products are for use in your business only and may not be resold or redistributed.

### Is this a physical product?

No. Everything in the shop is digital.

### Which listing should I start with?

If you offer one main service, start with the matching core bundle. If you offer both IV therapy and weight loss, start with the Complete Med Spa Bundle.
