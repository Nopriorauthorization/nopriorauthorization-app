GLP-1 stands for glucagon-like peptide-1 — a hormone your body already makes after you eat. In people struggling with weight, this signal is often weakened or ignored. Medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide mimic and amplify this signal, telling your brain: you're full. Stop eating.

Tirzepatide targets TWO pathways: GLP-1 and GIP. GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide) works with GLP-1 to amplify satiety and improve how your body processes fat. This dual mechanism is why tirzepatide clinical trials showed greater weight loss than semaglutide.

Same class, different power.

What happens to your blood sugar on GLP-1 therapy: These medications slow glucose absorption, stimulate insulin release when glucose is present, and reduce glucagon secretion. **9.**

Tirzepatide vs semaglutide — the question we get asked every day.

Which is right for you? Let's talk.

**3.**

Tirzepatide: Once weekly. Dual GLP-1/GIP agonist. Brand names include Mounjaro (diabetes) and Zepbound (weight loss).

Same class. Different mechanisms. Different results for different people.

We troubleshoot everything.
