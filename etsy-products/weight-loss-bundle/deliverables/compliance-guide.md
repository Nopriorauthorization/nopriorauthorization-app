# Medical Weight Loss Content Compliance Guide
## FTC & FDA Guidelines for Healthcare Providers
### No Prior Authorization | Hello Gorgeous Med Spa

---

## Overview

As a medical provider posting weight loss content on social media, you are held to higher standards than general wellness influencers. This guide summarizes key compliance requirements to protect your license and your patients.

---

## FTC Guidelines for Healthcare Providers

### 1. Testimonials & Endorsements

**Required:**
- Disclose material connections (if patient received free/discounted services)
- Include "Individual results may vary" or similar disclaimer
- Ensure testimonials reflect honest opinions and typical experiences

**Prohibited:**
- Fabricating testimonials
- Using testimonials that claim atypical results without disclosure
- Implying guaranteed outcomes

### 2. Before & After Photos

**Required:**
- Same lighting, angle, and conditions when possible
- Accurate representation (no editing that changes body shape)
- Clear timeframe disclosure
- "Individual results may vary" disclaimer

**Prohibited:**
- Misleading editing or filters
- Using stock photos as patient results
- Implying results are guaranteed or typical without evidence

### 3. Claims About Medications

**Required:**
- Accuracy in describing FDA-approved uses
- Distinguish between brand-name and compounded medications
- Disclose if discussing off-label use

**Prohibited:**
- Unsubstantiated efficacy claims
- Claims that contradict FDA labeling
- Promising specific weight loss amounts as guaranteed

## FDA Guidelines for Drug Advertising

### What You CAN Say:
- FDA-approved indications (weight management for BMI 30+ or 27+ with comorbidity)
- Published clinical trial data (with context)
- General mechanism of action
- Common side effects (balanced presentation)

### What You CANNOT Say:
- "Cures obesity"
- "No side effects" or minimizing known risks
- Specific weight loss guarantees ("You WILL lose 20 lbs")
- Comparative claims without substantiation ("Better than surgery")

### Required Disclosures:
- Major risks and contraindications (can link to full prescribing info)
- "See prescribing information for full details"
- Indication statement (what the drug is approved for)

## State Medical Board Considerations

### General Principles:
- Do not provide medical advice to non-patients via social media
- Maintain patient confidentiality (HIPAA)
- Avoid establishing patient relationships through comments/DMs
- Keep clear boundaries between education and consultation

### Patient Content:
- Obtain written consent for testimonials and photos
- Store consent forms securely
- Allow patients to revoke consent at any time
- Never pressure patients to provide testimonials

---

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram/Facebook (Meta):
- No "before and after" images that imply negative self-perception
- Restricted targeting for weight loss content (cannot target under 18)
- May require "Paid Partnership" label for sponsored content

### TikTok:
- No promotion of "dangerous weight loss behaviors"
- Medical content may be flagged for review
- Consider adding educational disclaimers

### General Best Practices:
- Use "#ad" or "#sponsored" for any paid content
- Disclose brand partnerships clearly
- Avoid making health claims in hashtags

## Template Disclaimer Language

### For Testimonial Posts:
```
*Individual results may vary. [Patient name/initials] received [treatment] at [practice name]. Results depend on individual factors including starting weight, health status, and adherence to protocol. This is not a guarantee of outcomes.*
```

### For Educational Posts:
```
*This content is for educational purposes only and is not medical advice. Consult with a licensed healthcare provider to determine if this treatment is right for you.*
```

### For Promotional Posts:
```
*[Medication name] is FDA-approved for chronic weight management in adults with BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with at least one weight-related condition. Not suitable for everyone. See prescribing information for risks and contraindications.*
```

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ "Guaranteed weight loss"
❌ "No side effects"
❌ "Works for everyone"
❌ "Lose X pounds in X weeks"
❌ "Better than [competitor/surgery/etc.]" without evidence
❌ "FDA approved for [unapproved use]"
❌ Showing patient photos without consent
❌ Diagnosing or treating via DMs/comments
❌ Making claims about compounded drugs that apply only to brand-name versions

---

## Green Light Language

✅ "May help with weight management"
✅ "Clinical trials showed [specific data with citation]"
✅ "Individual results vary"
✅ "Consult with a provider to see if you're a candidate"
✅ "FDA-approved for chronic weight management"
✅ "Common side effects include..."
✅ "We personalize protocols based on your health history"
✅ "Book a consultation to learn more"

## Checklist Before Posting

- [ ] Is every claim accurate and substantiated?
- [ ] Are disclaimers included where needed?
- [ ] Is patient consent documented (if using patient content)?
- [ ] Are side effects/risks mentioned or linked?
- [ ] Does this comply with my state board guidelines?
- [ ] Would I be comfortable if a regulator saw this post?
