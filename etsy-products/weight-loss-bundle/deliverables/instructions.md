# How to Use the Canva Templates

## Step 1: Access the Templates

1. Open `Canva_Templates_Access.pdf`
2. Click on the template link you want to use
3. You'll be taken to Canva with the option "Use this template"
4. Click "Use this template" — this creates a copy in YOUR Canva account

**Note:** You do NOT need Canva Pro. All templates use free elements and fonts.

## Step 2: Customize the Template

1. Click on any text to edit
2. Replace placeholder text with your content
3. Add your logo (upload to Canva if needed)
4. Adjust colors to match your brand (optional)
5. Replace photo placeholders with your own images (if applicable)

## Step 3: Download & Post

1. Click "Share" → "Download"
2. Choose PNG or JPG (PNG recommended for quality)
3. Upload to Instagram, Facebook, or your platform of choice

# Brand Customization Tips

## Matching Your Brand Colors

The templates use our brand palette:
- Hot Pink: #D4537E
- Black: #1A1A1A
- White: #FFFFFF
- Blush: #FBEAF0

**To change colors in Canva:**
1. Click on the element you want to change
2. Click the color square in the top toolbar
3. Enter your hex code or choose from the palette

## Adding Your Logo

1. Upload your logo to Canva (PNG with transparent background works best)
2. Drag it onto the template
3. Resize and position in the corner or footer
4. Lock the position if desired

## Font Substitutions

Templates use free Canva fonts:
- Headlines: Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond
- Body: Lato or Montserrat

Feel free to swap for your brand fonts (keep readability in mind).
