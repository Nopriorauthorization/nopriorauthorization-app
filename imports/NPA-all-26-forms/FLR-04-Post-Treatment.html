Dermal Filler
Aftercare Instructions
Please follow carefully for best results
Your results depend on how well you care for the treated area. Follow these instructions closely.
Immediately After
Apply ice gently — 10 min on, 10 min off for first hour
Do NOT massage or press on treated areas unless instructed
Remain upright — avoid lying face-down for 4 hours
Avoid touching face with unwashed hands
First 24 Hours
Avoid strenuous exercise
Avoid alcohol
Avoid saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs, hot yoga
Sleep on your back if possible
Tylenol for discomfort — avoid ibuprofen/aspirin
Lip Filler Specifically
Avoid kissing or using a straw for 24 hours
Avoid lip waxing or laser for 2 weeks
Contact us at first sign of cold sore outbreak
What Is Normal
Swelling — peaks 24–48 hrs, may last up to 2 weeks
Bruising — 1–2 weeks, especially lips and under-eyes
Firmness or lumpiness — softens within 2–4 weeks
Asymmetry — often resolves as swelling subsides

Assess final results at 2–4 weeks.

When to Call Us IMMEDIATELY
Blanching (white or pale area) near injection site
Skin that looks mottled, dusky, or blue/grey
Significant worsening pain after the first hour
Vision changes of any kind → GO TO ER IMMEDIATELY
Signs of infection: redness, warmth, pus, fever
