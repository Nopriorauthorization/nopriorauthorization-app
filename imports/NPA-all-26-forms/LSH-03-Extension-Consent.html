Fill

Lash extensions are individual synthetic, mink, or silk fibers adhered to each natural lash using semi-permanent adhesive. They are designed to last through the natural lash growth cycle (6–8 weeks) with fills every 2–4 weeks.

Full set duration90–120 minutes
Fill duration60–90 minutes
Fill scheduleEvery 2–3 weeks
Fill minimum40% retention required
Longevity6–8 weeks with proper care
Risks
Temporary redness or sensitivity during or after service
Eye irritation from adhesive fumes (temporary watering)
Allergic reaction to adhesive (see Patch Test Waiver)
Natural lash damage from improper home care or removal
Premature shedding with certain products or medications
Removal Policy

Extensions must be removed professionally. Self-removal or use of unapproved removers can cause permanent natural lash damage. Professional removal is available at Hello Gorgeous Med Spa.
