Things You'll Know After Reading This

The Knowledge
Your Provider Kept.

01
Why "your labs are normal" is not the same as optimal
A ferritin of 22 is "normal." It is also causing your hair to fall out and making you exhausted. Normal is a statistical range. Optimal is where symptoms resolve. Chapter 17 gives you the specific numbers.
02
The one question that reveals whether your injector knows what they're doing
Ask them to describe the vascular anatomy of the area they're about to inject. A well-trained injector answers immediately. An undertrained one hesitates. Chapter 11 gives you the whole vetting framework.
03
Why IPL is contraindicated for Fitzpatrick Types IV–VI — and what to use instead
It's physics. Broad-spectrum light absorbs at the epidermis before reaching the target in melanin-rich skin. The result: burns and 18-month PIH. The alternative: Nd:YAG 1064nm. Every provider should know this. Not all do.
04
What Botox cannot do — and why that distinction saves you thousands
Botox treats dynamic lines — lines caused by muscle movement. It does not treat static lines — lines present when your face is at rest. If your provider injects Botox into a nasolabial fold, ask why. The answer matters.
05
The muscle loss problem no one tells you about before starting GLP-1 therapy
Rapid weight loss depletes lean mass alongside fat — unless you hit 0.8–1g of protein per pound of bodyweight daily and resistance train 3x/week throughout your course. This is not optional. It changes everything about your outcome.
06
Why your skin is aging faster at 45 than it did at 42 — and what's actually driving it
Estrogen receptors are present throughout the skin. Collagen production is estrogen-dependent. As estrogen declines in perimenopause, collagen production declines with it — approximately 30% in the first five years. This is not just sun damage. This is hormonal. Chapter 15 explains the full picture.
