Pre-Treatment Instructions
Neurotoxin · Please read before your appointment
Follow these guidelines to minimize bruising, optimize results, and ensure a smooth experience. Questions? Call us before your appointment.
7 Days Before — Avoid These (increase bruising risk)
Aspirin and aspirin-containing products
Ibuprofen, naproxen, other NSAIDs
Fish oil, omega-3, flaxseed oil
Vitamin E supplements above 400 IU
Ginkgo biloba, garlic supplements, ginseng
Alcohol (especially within 24 hours)

Do NOT stop prescription blood thinners without your physician's guidance.

24 Hours Before
Avoid alcohol completely
Stay well hydrated — drink plenty of water
Avoid strenuous exercise the evening before
Get a good night's sleep
What to Eat

Eat a light meal or snack before your appointment. Staying nourished reduces lightheadedness. Avoid excessive caffeine the morning of your appointment.

Day of Appointment

Please arrive with:

Clean, makeup-free face (treatment areas)
No heavy creams, serums, or sunscreen on treatment areas
List of current medications if not already on file

Please reschedule if you have:

Active cold sore or open skin irritation
Facial waxing or hair removal same day
What to Expect

Your appointment takes 15–30 minutes. Most patients describe the sensation as a small pinch. Mild redness or swelling immediately after is completely normal and temporary.

Results timeline: Initial effects appear within 3–5 days. Full results at 10–14 days. Duration: 3–4 months on average. A complimentary 2-week follow-up is included.
