Pre-Treatment Instructions
Chemical Peel
Following these instructions carefully is essential for your safety and to achieve the best possible results from your chemical peel. Failure to comply may increase the risk of complications or diminish treatment effectiveness.
2 Weeks Before Your Peel
  • Discontinue all retinoids — including tretinoin (Retin-A), retinol, adapalene (Differin), and tazarotene (Tazorac). These thin the stratum corneum and increase the risk of over-penetration and irritation.
  • Stop AHA/BHA products — discontinue glycolic acid, salicylic acid, lactic acid, and any exfoliating toners or serums.
  • No waxing or depilatory creams — do not wax, thread, or use chemical hair removal on the treatment area. Shaving is permitted.
  • Avoid electrolysis — do not undergo electrolysis on or near the treatment area.
  • Begin daily broad-spectrum SPF 30+ — if not already part of your routine, begin applying sunscreen every morning to minimize UV-induced pigment activity before your peel.
1 Week Before Your Peel
  • Begin antiviral medication if prescribed — if you have any history of cold sores (HSV-1), your provider may prescribe valacyclovir (Valtrex) or acyclovir to begin 2–3 days before treatment and continue for 7–10 days after.
  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure — stay out of direct sunlight as much as possible. Sunburned skin cannot be treated.
  • No new skincare products — do not introduce any new products that could cause irritation or sensitization.
  • Moisturize daily — keep skin well-hydrated with a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer to optimize skin barrier function.
48 Hours Before Your Peel
  • No exfoliation of any kind — do not use scrubs, exfoliating brushes, microdermabrasion devices, or enzyme masks.
  • No tanning — avoid all tanning, whether natural sun exposure or artificial (tanning beds, spray tan, self-tanner).
  • Avoid laser or IPL treatments — do not undergo any laser, intense pulsed light, or energy-based skin treatments.
  • Discontinue benzoyl peroxide — stop all benzoyl peroxide products, which can increase dryness and sensitivity.
Day of Treatment
  • Arrive with a clean face — no makeup, sunscreen, serums, moisturizers, or any other products on the treatment area. Skin must be completely bare.
  • Avoid caffeine if you are sensitive — caffeine may heighten sensitivity to discomfort during the peel application.
  • Eat a light meal beforehand — do not arrive on an empty stomach, as mild lightheadedness can occasionally occur.
  • Bring sun protection for after your appointment — bring a wide-brimmed sun hat and a physical (mineral) SPF 30+ to apply after treatment. Your skin will be extremely sensitive to UV immediately post-peel.
Rescheduling Policy: Your appointment must be rescheduled if you arrive with a sunburn, active breakout with open lesions, an active cold sore, or any sign of skin infection on the treatment area. Please contact us at 630-636-6193 as early as possible to reschedule.
