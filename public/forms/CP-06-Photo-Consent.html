Patient Information
Purpose of Photography

Before-and-after photographs are an important part of documenting your treatment progress and results. These images provide a visual record of your skin's condition prior to treatment and the improvement achieved over the course of your care plan. Photographs may also be used for educational or promotional purposes, but only with your explicit consent as indicated below. You have the right to choose exactly how your photographs may be used.

Consent Tiers — Select All That Apply
Please check each tier you consent to. You may select one or more tiers. Selecting a higher tier does not automatically include lower tiers — please check each one individually.
Tier 1 — Clinical Record Only
Photographs will be stored in my patient chart and used exclusively for clinical documentation, treatment planning, and tracking my personal treatment progress. Images will not be shared outside the clinic.
Tier 2 — Educational Use
De-identified photographs (with no visible identifying features such as full face, name, or date of birth) may be used for internal staff training, continuing education presentations, and professional educational materials. All identifying information will be removed or obscured.
Tier 3 — Marketing & Website
Photographs may be used on the clinic website, printed brochures, in-office display materials, and other promotional content. Face may be visible in these images. My name will not be used unless I provide separate written permission.
Tier 4 — Social Media
Photographs may be posted on clinic social media accounts, including but not limited to Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Face may be visible. I understand that once published on social media, images may be shared, screenshot, or reposted by third parties beyond the clinic's control.
Photo Guidelines
Standardized Photography Protocol All clinical photographs will be taken using standardized lighting conditions and consistent patient positioning to ensure accurate comparison between sessions. Photographs will be taken from multiple angles as appropriate for the treatment area. No identifying information (name, date of birth) will appear within the image frame for Tier 2 de-identified use.
Patient Rights

Right to revoke consentYou may revoke this consent at any time by submitting a written request to Hello Gorgeous Med Spa. Revocation will apply to future use only.
