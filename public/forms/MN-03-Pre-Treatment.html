Pre-Treatment
Instructions
Microneedling
Following these instructions carefully will help maximize your results and minimize the risk of complications. Please review each section and contact our office if you have any questions.
2 Weeks Before Treatment
▸ Discontinue all retinoids including tretinoin (Retin-A), retinol serums, adapalene (Differin), and tazarotene (Tazorac). These thin the epidermis and increase sensitivity.
▸ Stop using alpha-hydroxy acids (AHA), beta-hydroxy acids (BHA), glycolic acid, salicylic acid, and lactic acid products. These exfoliants compromise the skin barrier.
▸ Avoid prolonged or unprotected sun exposure. If you must be outdoors, apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every 2 hours. Sunburned skin cannot be treated.
▸ Do not wax, use depilatory creams, or undergo electrolysis on the treatment area. Shaving is permitted.
▸ No laser treatments, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, or dermaplaning in the treatment area.
▸ If you have a history of cold sores (HSV-1), notify your provider. Prophylactic antiviral medication (Valacyclovir) may be prescribed to begin 2 days before treatment.
48 Hours Before Treatment
▸ Avoid non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin), naproxen (Aleve), and aspirin. These increase the risk of bruising and prolonged bleeding. Acetaminophen (Tylenol) is acceptable if needed.
▸ Do not consume alcohol, as it acts as a blood thinner and may increase bruising and swelling.
▸ Stop using exfoliating cleansers, scrubs, or facial brushes (Clarisonic, etc.).
▸ Discontinue vitamin E supplements and fish oil, which may increase bruising tendency.
▸ Avoid any new skincare products that have not been previously tested on your skin.
Day of Treatment
▸ Arrive with completely clean skin — no makeup, sunscreen, moisturizer, serums, or tinted products. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser before arriving, or plan to cleanse at the office.
▸ Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing, especially tops that do not need to be pulled over your face.
▸ Eat a light meal or snack before your appointment to prevent lightheadedness during treatment.
▸ Stay well hydrated — drink plenty of water in the hours leading up to your appointment.
▸ If topical numbing cream was prescribed, apply it to the treatment area 30–45 minutes before your appointment time as directed.
What to Bring
List of current medications and supplements
Sunglasses (if treating around the eye area)
Gentle cleanser and hydrating moisturizer for post-care
Wide-brimmed hat or scarf for sun protection on the way home
Completed consultation and consent forms (if not already on file)
Any prescribed antiviral or prophylactic medication
Important — Your appointment may need to be rescheduled if any of the following are present on the day of treatment: active acne breakout or pustules in the treatment area, sunburn or windburn, active cold sore or herpes outbreak, open wounds, cuts, or abrasions, signs of skin infection (redness, swelling, warmth, discharge), recent Botox or filler injection within 2 weeks.
