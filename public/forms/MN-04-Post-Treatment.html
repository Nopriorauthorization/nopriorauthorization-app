Post-Treatment
Aftercare
Microneedling
Your skin has thousands of micro-channels that are actively healing. Following these aftercare instructions is critical for optimal results, proper healing, and infection prevention. Keep this sheet for reference throughout your recovery.
Immediately After Treatment
▸ Your skin will appear red and flushed, similar to a moderate sunburn. This is a normal and expected response.
▸ You may experience warmth, tightness, and a mild stinging sensation in the treated area.
▸ Mild swelling is expected, particularly around the eyes and forehead, and will subside within 24–48 hours.
▸ Pinpoint bleeding spots may be visible — this indicates an effective treatment depth and will resolve quickly.
▸ Your provider will apply a hydrating serum (hyaluronic acid or growth factor) immediately after treatment. Leave this on as directed.
First 24 Hours
▸ Do not touch your face with unwashed hands. The micro-channels are open and susceptible to bacteria.
▸ No makeup, tinted sunscreen, or active skincare products (retinol, vitamin C, AHAs, BHAs, benzoyl peroxide).
▸ No exercise, hot yoga, saunas, steam rooms, or activities that cause sweating. Sweat contains bacteria and salt that can irritate open channels.
▸ Use only a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser if needed (such as CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser or La Roche-Posay Toleriane). Pat dry — do not rub.
▸ Apply the provided hydrating serum or plain hyaluronic acid serum as directed by your provider.
▸ Sleep on a clean silk or satin pillowcase to minimize friction and bacterial exposure.
▸ Avoid direct sun exposure. Stay indoors when possible.
Days 2–3
▸ Redness should begin to subside noticeably. Skin may feel dry, tight, or slightly rough.
▸ Continue using gentle cleanser morning and evening. Follow with hydrating serum and a fragrance-free moisturizer.
▸ Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher before any outdoor exposure. Mineral (zinc oxide) sunscreens are preferred, as they are less irritating than chemical sunscreens.
▸ Do not use exfoliating products, acids, scrubs, or retinoids.
▸ Mineral makeup may be applied gently if necessary, but clean application tools are essential.
▸ Avoid touching or picking at the skin, even if it feels rough or begins to peel.
Days 4–7
▸ Mild flaking or peeling may occur as the skin sheds. This is a normal part of the renewal process — do not pick, peel, or scratch.
▸ Continue your gentle cleanser, hydrating serum, moisturizer, and SPF routine.
▸ Avoid swimming pools, hot tubs, saunas, and natural bodies of water (lakes, ocean). Chlorine and bacteria can cause irritation or infection.
▸ Do not reintroduce retinoids, vitamin C serums, or exfoliating acids during this phase.
▸ Light exercise may be resumed after day 3, but shower immediately afterward and avoid touching your face.
Week 2 and Beyond
▸ Gradually reintroduce active products: start with vitamin C serum, then retinol (at a lower strength initially).
▸ Continue daily SPF 30+ application — this is essential for protecting new collagen and preventing hyperpigmentation.
▸ Your skin may begin to appear brighter, smoother, and more even-toned. Full collagen remodeling continues for 3–6 months.
▸ Resume your full skincare routine as tolerated, reintroducing one active product at a time.
▸ Schedule your next session 4–6 weeks from today for optimal series results.
When to Contact the Clinic
Seek immediate attention if you experience any of the following: Increasing redness, pain, or swelling after 72 hours · Pus, yellow/green discharge, or crusting · Warmth or fever suggesting infection · Severe swelling, especially around the eyes or lips · Signs of allergic reaction (hives, difficulty breathing, widespread rash) · Cold sore outbreak or blistering · Any symptom that seems unusual or concerning
