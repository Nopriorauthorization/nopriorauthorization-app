Card details — Dermal Filler Aftercare

Filler aftercare is more involved than Botox — more instructions, longer recovery window. Consider a slightly longer card (5×7) or a folded card. Print on card stock for durability. Always include the vascular occlusion warning — this is your legal protection.

No Prior Authorization  ·  Your Clinic Name

Filler Aftercare

What to expect in the next 4 weeks

First 24 hours

  • Apply cold compress 10 min on/off for swelling
  • Sleep elevated — extra pillow under head
  • No makeup over treated areas
  • No alcohol — increases swelling and bruising
  • No blood thinners (aspirin, ibuprofen)

First 2 weeks — avoid

  • Facials, massage, or pressure on face
  • Dental procedures
  • Extreme heat or cold — saunas, skiing
  • High-intensity exercise first 24–48 hrs

What to expect

  • Swelling and bruising: normal 3–7 days
  • Final result visible: 2–4 weeks
  • Lips may feel firm initially — softens

⚠️ Contact us immediately if you notice

  • Blanching (white skin) near injection site
  • Severe pain, mottled or blue-gray skin
  • Vision changes of any kind

The vascular occlusion warning

The "contact us immediately" section on this card is not optional. It is your clinical and legal protection. A patient who experiences blanching post-filler and doesn't know what it means may wait hours before calling. This card tells them to call immediately — which is what makes the difference between a manageable complication and a catastrophic one.

Lips-specific note

Lip filler patients need extra reassurance in the first 72 hours — lips swell more dramatically than other areas and patients frequently panic. Consider a separate "Lip Filler" card variant with a lips-specific swelling timeline. The Canva template includes both.

Card details — Microneedling Aftercare

Microneedling patients need the most aftercare education of any service — the "don't use anything active" window is critical and often violated. This card is slightly more text-heavy than others. Consider a folded 4×6 or a 5×7 single card.

Microneedling Aftercare

Protect your results in the next 72 hours

Today and tomorrow

  • Cleanse gently with lukewarm water only
  • Apply barrier cream (Aquaphor) as needed
  • Mineral SPF 30+ — no chemical sunscreen yet
  • No makeup for 24 hours
  • No gym or sweating for 48 hours
  • No hot showers or steam rooms

First 72 hours — NO actives

  • No retinoids or retinol
  • No AHAs or BHAs (glycolic, salicylic)
  • No vitamin C serum
  • No niacinamide or strong actives
  • No exfoliation of any kind

What to expect

  • Redness and warmth: 24–48 hours normal
  • Tightness and mild flaking: days 3–5
  • Visible improvement: begins week 2–4
  • Full collagen result: 3–6 months

The active ingredients window

The most common microneedling complication is patients going home and applying their full skincare routine — retinol, vitamin C, acids — onto freshly needled skin. The card makes this explicit and impossible to miss. Every patient who reads it knows exactly what not to do.

The collagen timeline conversation

Include this verbally and on the card: results continue improving for 3–6 months after the last session. Patients who understand this don't expect dramatic transformation after one treatment and are consistently more satisfied with their outcomes.

Card details — Chemical Peel Aftercare

Chemical peel patients have the most anxiety post-treatment — especially medium-depth peels. The card needs to normalize the peeling process and prevent picking. Bold the "do not pick" instruction — it is the most important thing on this card.

No Prior Authorization  ·  Your Clinic Name

Chemical Peel Aftercare

Days 1–14 — what to expect and what to do

Days 1–3

  • Cleanse gently — non-foaming, fragrance-free only
  • Apply barrier cream frequently (Aquaphor)
  • SPF 50 every morning — reapply if outside
  • No makeup
  • No retinoids, AHAs, BHAs, vitamin C

Days 3–7 — the peeling phase

  • Skin will flake and peel — this is normal
  • DO NOT PICK, PULL, OR PEEL the skin
  • Do not force flaking — let it shed naturally
  • Moisturize more frequently to ease tightness

Always avoid

  • Sun exposure — wear a hat outdoors
  • Steam rooms, saunas, hot baths
  • Strenuous exercise until fully healed

Your result

  • Visible improvement: week 2
  • Next peel: 4–6 weeks minimum

The picking warning — make it unmissable

In the Canva template, "DO NOT PICK, PULL, OR PEEL" is in a larger font and a contrasting color. This instruction is broken more than any other peel aftercare instruction — and when it is, it causes scarring and hyperpigmentation. Make it visually impossible to miss.

Sun protection is the result protector

A patient who skips SPF after a chemical peel undoes a significant portion of their result. The card includes this twice — in the daily instructions and in the "always avoid" section. Reinforce it verbally at checkout as well.

CHEMICAL PEEL AFTERCARE — Hello Gorgeous Med Spa | No Prior Authorization DAYS 1–3: ✓ Cleanse gently twice daily with a non-foaming, fragrance-free cleanser ✓ Apply barrier cream (Aquaphor or CeraVe Healing Ointment) frequently ✓ SPF 50 every single morning — reapply every 2 hours if outdoors ✗ No makeup until skin has fully healed ✗ No retinoids, AHAs, BHAs, vitamin C, or any active ingredients DAYS 3–7 — THE PEELING PHASE: ✓ Skin will begin to flake and peel — this is normal and means the peel is working ✗ DO NOT PICK, PULL, OR PEEL the flaking skin under any circumstances ✗ Do not force or speed up the shedding — let it happen naturally ✓ Moisturize more frequently to ease tightness and support the healing process ALWAYS AVOID DURING HEALING: ✗ Direct sun exposure — wear SPF 50 and a wide-brim hat outdoors ✗ Steam rooms, saunas, hot baths, or steam showers ✗ Strenuous exercise until skin has fully healed and stopped peeling ✗ Picking or touching the face unnecessarily YOUR RESULT TIMELINE: ✓ Visible improvement begins: end of week 2 ✓ Minimum time before next peel: 4–6 weeks ✓ For best results: series of 3–6 peels spaced 4–6 weeks apart Questions? Card details — PMU Healing Timeline Card

This is your most important aftercare card. The ghost phase is the #1 cause of panic and negative reviews in PMU. The card makes the entire healing journey visual and expected. Consider a folded card with the timeline on one side and aftercare on the other.

Your PMU Healing Journey

What to expect — day by day
Days 1–3
Dark & Bold

Brows appear 30–50% darker than final result. Slight swelling normal. Blot gently — do not rub.

Days 4–7
Flaking & Peeling

Skin begins shedding. DO NOT PICK. Picking removes pigment and causes scarring.

Days 8–21
The Ghost Phase

Brows may look patchy, faded, or nearly invisible. THIS IS NORMAL. Pigment is still there — hidden under new skin forming. Do not panic.

Weeks 4–6
Color Resurfaces

Pigment gradually reappears as new skin thins. Your result begins to show.

Week 6–8
Healed Result

True result is visible. Your touch-up appointment is scheduled for this window.

The ghost phase — say it out loud and put it on the card

"Days 8–21 your brows may look patchy or nearly invisible. This is completely normal — it's called the ghost phase." When clients see this printed on a card they received on day one, they trust the process instead of calling you in a panic on day 12.

Back of card

IV Therapy Aftercare

How to maximize your results today

Right now — today

  • Drink at least 64oz of water today
  • Keep bandage on IV site for 1 hour
  • Eat a light meal if you haven't already
  • No alcohol for 24 hours
  • No strenuous exercise today

Watch for and contact us if you notice

  • Redness, swelling, or warmth at IV site after 24 hrs
  • Fever or chills developing within 24 hours
  • Hardness or a cord-like feeling at the IV site

What you may notice

  • Bright yellow urine — normal with B vitamins
  • Energy increase within 1–4 hours
  • Small bruise at IV site — resolves in a few days
  • Slight taste of vitamins — normal during infusion

Ready for your next drip?

The membership prompt

The bottom of this card mentions your membership program. Every IV client holds this card. The membership mention turns an aftercare card into a passive marketing tool — without feeling pushy. Include your membership pricing or a QR code to your membership page on the back.

The bright urine warning

B vitamins turn urine bright yellow-orange. Without warning, patients are alarmed. With warning, they laugh about it. This one sentence on the card prevents unnecessary anxiety and creates a memorable, humanizing moment with your practice.
