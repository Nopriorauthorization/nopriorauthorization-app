Use preservative-free 0.9% NS. Bacteriostatic NS acceptable per provider preference. Roll vial — never shake. Refrigerate 2–8°C. Use within 4 weeks of reconstitution.
🧴 Storage & Handling
BEFORE
Store unopened at 2–8°C (refrigerated). Keep from light. Do not freeze.
AFTER
Reconstituted: refrigerate. Botox/Jeuveau up to 4 wks. Xeomin up to 4 wks. Dysport up to 4 hrs per label (most use 24 hrs).
NEVER
Do not use if discolored, particulate matter visible, or vial seal compromised.
TEMP
Safe up to 25°C for up to 5 days per Allergan data. Use clinical judgment.
🚫 Contraindications
ABS
Absolute: Known hypersensitivity to BTX-A or albumin. Infection at injection site. Myasthenia gravis, Eaton-Lambert syndrome, ALS.
REL
Relative: Pregnancy / breastfeeding. Neuromuscular disorders. Blood thinners / anticoagulants. Recent facial surgery (<2 wks). Keloidal scarring tendency.
MED
Drug interactions ↑ effect: Aminoglycosides, quinidine, chloroquine, cyclosporine, penicillamine, lincomycin.

🎯 Injection Depth & Technique
UPPER
Glabella/forehead: Intradermal to subcutaneous. Inject superior to orbital rim for forehead to avoid brow ptosis.
MID
Crow's feet: Subcutaneous, lateral to orbit. Stay ≥1cm from orbital rim. Fan technique at each point.
LOWER
Lip flip/DAO/mentalis: Superficial intradermal — orbicularis is superficial. Small volumes (0.05–0.1mL/point).
NECK
Platysma: Pinch and inject intramuscularly. Avoid deep — risk to carotid, jugular, thyroid.
MASSETER
IM injection. Have patient clench — identify bulk. Inject into central mass. Avoid parotid gland (anterior border of masseter).
⚠️ Complication Management
PTOSIS
Lid ptosis (droopy eyelid): Apraclonidine 0.5% eye drops (Iopidine) — stimulates Müller's muscle. 1–2 drops TID. Resolves with toxin metabolism (4–12 wks).
BROW
Brow ptosis: Educate patient — resolves spontaneously. No reversal agent. Avoid repeating the same injection pattern. Consider Apraclonidine for compensatory lid opening.
ASYM
Asymmetry: Wait until day 14 for full effect. Touch-up with 1–2 units on weaker side. Document pre-existing asymmetry at consult.
BRUISE
Bruising: Arnica gel/supplements. Avoid aspirin/NSAIDs pre-treatment. Cold compress immediately post-injection. Resolves 5–14 days.
HEADACHE
Post-injection headache: Common within 24–48hrs. Tylenol (avoid NSAIDs x24hrs). Ice pack. Usually resolves spontaneously.
SPREAD
Unwanted spread: Dysphagia, diplopia, dysphonia — rare but serious. Document, monitor, supportive care. Refer to ER if dysphagia is severe.
ALLERGY
Anaphylaxis: Epi 0.3mg IM (EpiPen). Call 911. Have epi on-site at all times. Extremely rare — botulinum protein allergy.
RESIST
Antibody resistance: Switch to Xeomin (no accessory proteins). Extend intervals. Minimize dose per session. Consider alternative toxin brand.

FACTORS
Duration affected by: metabolism, muscle mass, exercise frequency, zinc levels, stress, immune status. Athletes and high-metabolism patients wear off faster.
ZINC
Zinc + phytase supplementation may extend duration. Zytaze (zinc + phytase) take 4 days before treatment. Evidence limited but low risk.
✅ Pre-Treatment Checklist
48HR
Hold: aspirin, NSAIDs, fish oil, vitamin E, garlic, ginger, turmeric supplements. Alcohol 24hr prior.
LABS
Not required for standard cosmetic use. Required for therapeutic use (hyperhidrosis, migraine, spasticity) per payer.
PHOTO
Always photograph: anterior, bilateral obliques, lateral. Document pre-existing asymmetry. Patient signs photo consent.
SCREEN
Screen: neuromuscular disease, current illness/fever, pregnancy, recent toxin (<3 months), antibiotics (aminoglycosides).
CONSENT
Informed consent: risks, alternatives, no guarantee of results. Review pre and post care verbally AND in writing.
🔬 Advanced Techniques & Clinical Pearls
BROW LIFT
1–2u lateral frontalis above tail of brow. Relaxes depressors (orbicularis, corrugator) — brow elevates medially. Avoid mid-frontalis — causes Spock brow.
NEFERTITI
Neck lift: Platysmal band injections + lower face border. 25–50u total. Tightens lower jaw definition. Results subtle — manage expectations.
FLIP
Lip flip: 4–6u orbicularis oris (upper lip). Result: slight eversion/curl of upper lip. NOT a volume replacement. Warn: temporary difficulty with straws, kissing, wind instruments.
MASSET
Masseter reduction: 20–30u/side. Results seen at 4–6 weeks. Full slimming at 3 months. Repeat 2–3x before maintenance interval lengthens.
TRAPEZIUS
Trap tox (Barbie shot): 50–100u/trap. Pain relief + shoulder slimming. Use grid injection pattern. Insurance may cover for medical indication.
COMBO
Tox + filler synergy: Treat dynamic lines with tox first. Wait 2 weeks, then address residual static lines with filler. Better results, longer duration.
📋 Post-Care Instructions (Patient-Facing)
4HR
Stay upright. No lying down. No bending at waist. No rubbing or massaging treated areas.
24HR
No strenuous exercise. No alcohol. No heat exposure (sauna, steam, direct sun). No facial.
2WK
Full results at day 14. Normal to have: mild bruising, asymmetry, heaviness. NOT normal: lid drooping, difficulty swallowing.
REBOOK
Most patients: 3–4 month interval. Send rebook text at week 10. Consistent scheduling = longer-lasting results over time.

🧠 High-Yield Clinical Pearls
★
Conservative first visit always. Under-treat and touch up at 2 weeks. Never over-treat a new patient.
★
Photograph every patient every visit. Protects you legally. Helps patient see results objectively.
★
Document pre-existing asymmetry at consultation and have patient acknowledge it on consent form.
★
Never treat above the periosteum in the glabellar area — risk of spreading to levator palpebrae → ptosis.
★
Wait 14 days before any touch-up. Tox continues to work for 2 weeks post-injection.
★
Dysport ratio: 2.5:1 to 3:1 vs Botox. Never assume equal dosing between brands.
★
Antibody resistance risk increases with high-dose, high-frequency injections. Minimum effective dose, maximum interval.
★
Epi on-site always. Anaphylaxis is extremely rare but requires immediate response.
