Key Rules — Never Forget
★ Never inject forehead within 2–3cm of orbital rim medially or 1.5cm laterally — ptosis risk
★ Always include procerus when treating glabella — incomplete treatment causes persistent horizontal lines
★ DAO must be injected at mandibular border — NOT at the modiolus — smile asymmetry is the #1 DAO complication
★ Masseter: have patient clench to identify muscle bulk. Never inject anterior to the masseter's anterior border — parotid
★ Assess at 14 days. Never judge Botox results before 14 days — results not complete until then
