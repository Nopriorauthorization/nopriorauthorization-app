Key Reminders
★ HA fillers (Juvederm, Restylane, Revanesse, Belotero) are REVERSIBLE with hyaluronidase. Sculptra and Radiesse are NOT.
★ G-prime = resistance to deformation. High G-prime = more lift and structural support. Low G-prime = softer, more natural movement.
★ All HA fillers cause swelling — assess results at 14 days minimum. Never add more filler before 14-day review.
★ Vycross (Juvederm) vs NASHA (Restylane): different HA crosslinking technologies — affects how the product moves and integrates.
