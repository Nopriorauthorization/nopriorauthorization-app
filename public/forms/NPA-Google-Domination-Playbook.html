Botox & Neurotoxins — 10 questions
How long does Botox last?▾
For most patients, Botox lasts 3 to 4 months. With consistent treatment over time, many patients find their results last longer as the treated muscles gradually soften. We'll help you build a maintenance schedule that keeps your results continuous and your appearance natural.
Does Botox hurt?▾
Most patients describe Botox as a small, quick pinch — far less uncomfortable than they expected. We use very fine needles and work efficiently. If you're anxious about discomfort, let us know and we can discuss options to make you more comfortable.
How much does Botox cost?▾
Botox is typically priced per unit or per area. At [Practice Name], we charge [your pricing]. During your consultation, we'll let you know exactly how many units we recommend for your goals so there are no surprises. We always provide a clear treatment plan before we begin.
When will I see results from Botox?▾
Botox begins working within 3 to 7 days and reaches its full effect around day 14. It's completely normal for results to appear gradually or unevenly at first — this is part of the natural process. We include a complimentary 2-week follow-up with every Botox appointment to assess your results and make any fine adjustments.
What should I avoid before Botox?▾
For 24 hours before your appointment: avoid alcohol, hold aspirin and ibuprofen if possible, skip fish oil and vitamin E supplements, and arrive with a clean face. We'll send you a personalized prep text 48 hours before your appointment with everything you need to know.
What's the difference between Botox and Dysport?▾
Both are FDA-approved botulinum toxin type A products that relax muscles to reduce wrinkles. The main differences are in their unit measurements, diffusion radius, and onset time. Dysport tends to spread slightly more and may kick in a day or two faster. Your provider will recommend the best option based on your anatomy and treatment areas.
Can I get Botox if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?▾
No — Botox is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. This is a standard contraindication we screen for at every appointment. We're happy to schedule your treatment after this period and will plan ahead with you.
Will Botox make me look frozen or unnatural?▾
Not when done correctly. We are a conservative practice — we believe in natural results that make you look refreshed, not done. We always start with a thoughtful dose and assess at your 2-week follow-up. You will never leave our practice looking overdone.
How many units of Botox do I need?▾
The number of units varies by area and individual muscle mass. On average: forehead 10–20 units, glabella (11s) 20–25 units, crow's feet 6–12 units per side. Your provider will assess your muscles during the consultation and give you an exact recommendation before anything is injected.
What can't I do after Botox?▾
For the first 24 hours: stay upright (no lying flat for 4 hours), skip the gym, avoid alcohol, and don't rub or massage the treated areas. We'll send you a detailed aftercare text immediately after your appointment so you have everything in writing.
Dermal Fillers — 8 questions
How long does filler last?▾
Depending on the area and product used, filler typically lasts 6 to 18 months. Lip filler tends to dissolve faster (6–12 months) while cheek and jawline filler can last 12–18 months or longer. Lifestyle factors like metabolism and sun exposure also affect longevity.
Does filler hurt?▾
Most dermal fillers contain lidocaine, which numbs the area as the product is injected. We also apply topical numbing cream 20–30 minutes before treatment. Most patients rate the discomfort as very manageable — lips tend to be the most sensitive area.
How much does lip filler cost?▾
Lip filler is typically priced per syringe. At [Practice Name], lip filler starts at [your price] per syringe. Most patients start with half to one syringe for a natural enhancement. We'll show you what we recommend during your consultation before we begin.
Can filler be reversed?▾
Yes — hyaluronic acid fillers (the most commonly used type) can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase. This is one of the reasons we use HA fillers as our primary choice — the reversibility adds an important safety layer. If you're ever unhappy with a result, we have options.
How soon can I see filler results?▾
You'll see results immediately, but swelling and bruising in the first 1–2 weeks can affect the appearance. Final results are visible at 4 weeks once all swelling has fully resolved. We schedule a follow-up assessment at this point to evaluate and fine-tune if needed.
What should I avoid after filler?▾
For the first 24–48 hours: no alcohol, no strenuous exercise, no heat exposure (saunas, steam rooms), no pressure or massage on treated areas, and no dental procedures. Apply cold compress gently if swelling occurs. We'll send you detailed aftercare instructions immediately after your appointment.
What is the difference between Botox and filler?▾
Botox relaxes muscles to reduce dynamic wrinkles (lines from movement). Filler adds volume to areas that have lost fullness or to enhance features. They treat different concerns and are frequently used together for comprehensive results. Your provider will explain which is appropriate for your goals during your consultation.
Is filler safe?▾
When performed by a trained, licensed provider, HA filler is very safe. Risks include bruising, swelling, asymmetry, and in rare cases vascular complications. We take every precaution — including mapping injection points before we begin and keeping hyaluronidase on-site at all times. Safety is never an afterthought here.
GLP-1 / Weight Loss — 6 questions
Do you offer semaglutide or Ozempic for weight loss?▾
Yes — we offer a medically supervised GLP-1 weight loss program including semaglutide and tirzepatide. Our program is led by [Provider Name], FNP-BC, and includes monthly provider visits, dose management, and ongoing support. Complimentary consultations are available to determine if you're a candidate.
How much weight can I lose on semaglutide?▾
Clinical studies show an average of 10–15% body weight loss with semaglutide over 68 weeks. Results vary based on starting weight, dose, diet, and activity level. We'll set realistic expectations with you at your consultation and track your progress at every monthly visit.
What are the side effects of GLP-1 medications?▾
The most common side effects are nausea, constipation, and fatigue — primarily during the first 4–8 weeks as your body adjusts. We start at the lowest dose and titrate slowly to minimize side effects. Most patients find them very manageable, and our team is available to help you through any difficult periods.
Do I qualify for GLP-1 weight loss medication?▾
FDA criteria include a BMI of 30 or above, or a BMI of 27 or above with at least one weight-related health condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, or high cholesterol. The best way to find out is a complimentary consultation — we'll review your history and let you know exactly what we can offer.
How much does the weight loss program cost?▾
Our GLP-1 program begins with an initiation visit at [price], which includes your full consultation, labs review, and injection training. Monthly membership is [price] and covers your monthly provider visit, dose management, and direct messaging support. Medication is billed separately through your pharmacy.
What happens when I stop taking GLP-1 medication?▾
Most patients regain weight when they stop GLP-1 medication without a maintenance plan. This is why we discuss long-term strategy from the start — including lower maintenance doses, lifestyle habits, and ongoing support. We're not here for a quick fix. We're here for lasting results.
IV Therapy, Skincare & General — 16 questions
What is IV therapy and what are the benefits?▾
IV therapy delivers vitamins, minerals, and hydration directly into your bloodstream — bypassing the digestive system for 100% absorption. Benefits depend on the formula chosen and can include improved energy, immune support, enhanced hydration, skin brightening, and faster recovery. We customize every drip to your goals.
How often should I get IV therapy?▾
Most patients start with weekly sessions for the first month, then move to biweekly or monthly maintenance. Our membership program makes regular IV therapy affordable — ask us about it at your visit.
What is microneedling and does it work?▾
Microneedling creates tiny, controlled micro-injuries in the skin that trigger your body's natural collagen production. It is clinically proven to improve skin texture, reduce acne scarring, minimize pores, and improve overall skin quality. Results are cumulative — most patients see the best results after a series of 3–6 treatments.
How long does microneedling take to work?▾
You may notice an initial glow within the first week, but the real results — collagen remodeling — develop over 3 to 6 months after your last session. This is why we recommend a series and take before photos — so you can see your full transformation over time.
What is a chemical peel?▾
A chemical peel uses an acidic solution to exfoliate the outer layers of skin, revealing smoother, brighter skin underneath. Peels range from superficial (no downtime) to medium-depth (5–7 days of peeling). We select the appropriate peel for your Fitzpatrick skin type and concerns — safety is our first priority.
Do you accept insurance?▾
Most aesthetic services are elective and not covered by insurance. However, some medically necessary treatments may be partially covered — ask us at your consultation. We accept all major credit cards, HSA/FSA accounts, and offer financing through [your financing partner].
How do I book a consultation?▾
You can book directly online at [your booking link], call or text us at [your phone], or send us a DM on Instagram @[handle]. Consultations are complimentary and available in person or virtually. We typically have availability within [timeframe] — reach out and we'll find you a time.
What should I expect at my first appointment?▾
Plan for 45–60 minutes. We'll start with a thorough consultation — reviewing your goals, health history, and any questions you have. We take baseline photos before any treatment begins. Nothing happens without your full understanding and explicit agreement. You'll leave with written aftercare instructions and a follow-up appointment scheduled.
Are your providers licensed and certified?▾
Yes. [Provider Name] is a [credentials] licensed in [state] with specialized training in aesthetic medicine. All providers at [Practice Name] are licensed in their scope of practice and maintain current certifications. We're happy to share our credentials — just ask.
Do you offer a membership or loyalty program?▾
Yes — our membership program starts at [price]/month and includes [benefits]. Members save on every visit, get priority scheduling, and receive exclusive access to new services and events. Ask us about membership at your next appointment or visit our website to learn more.
What is microblading?▾
Microblading is a semi-permanent cosmetic procedure that uses a manual blade to create hair-like strokes of pigment in the brow area — giving the appearance of fuller, more defined brows. Results last 12–18 months. Every microblading service includes a perfecting touch-up at 6–8 weeks.
Is there downtime after microblading?▾
There is a healing process — not traditional "downtime." Your brows will be darker for the first 3 days, then begin to flake and peel (do not pick). Between days 8 and 21, brows may look patchy or faded — this is called the ghost phase and is completely normal. Your healed result appears at 6–8 weeks.
Can I bring a friend to my appointment?▾
Of course — especially for your first visit! Many patients feel more comfortable with a support person. We just ask that guests wait in our reception area during the treatment itself to keep the environment calm and focused for you.
What is your cancellation policy?▾
We ask for [24/48]-hour notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a [amount] fee, which is applied toward your next appointment. We understand life happens — just communicate with us and we'll always work with you.
Do you offer gift cards?▾
Yes! Gift cards are available in any amount and make a thoughtful gift for any occasion. You can purchase them in person or [online at your website]. They're valid for any service or retail product and never expire.
What makes your med spa different from others?▾
We are a provider-owned, patient-first practice. Every treatment plan is built by [Provider Name], [credentials] — not a salesperson. We start conservatively, follow up with every patient, and never do more than what you actually need. Our results speak for themselves — but so do our reviews. We invite you to come in and feel the difference.

52 done-for-you post templates — one for every week of the year

Why Google Posts matter for your ranking

Google rewards active profiles. Profiles that post regularly appear higher in local search results than inactive ones. Posts expire after 7 days — which means weekly posting is required to keep your profile "active" in Google's eyes. Most med spas never post. This is a free ranking advantage hiding in plain sight.

The 4 post types — rotate weekly
Week 1 — Educational

Teach something about one of your services. Answers a question patients commonly ask. Builds trust and authority.

Week 2 — Promotional

Feature a service, highlight a special offer, or spotlight a package. Include a "Book Now" button.

Week 3 — Social proof

Share a patient result (with consent), highlight a review, or showcase a before/after. Let results speak.

Week 4 — Behind the scenes

Team photo, clinic moment, new product arrival, event announcement. Humanizes the practice.

Website SEO Checklist

25 fixes ranked by impact — all doable inside your existing website platform

Critical fixes — do these first
Page titles include your city and service — e.g. "Botox in Oswego IL | Hello Gorgeous Med Spa" — not just "Home" or your practice name
Every service has its own dedicated page — Botox, filler, IV therapy, GLP-1, skincare — each gets a separate URL, not one giant "Services" page
Your city name appears in the first paragraph of your homepage — Google needs to know where you are
Your NAP is consistent — Name, Address, Phone number must be identical on your website, Google profile, and every directory listing
Your website is mobile-friendly — test at search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly — over 70% of med spa searches happen on phones
Your website loads in under 3 seconds — test at pagespeed.web.dev — slow sites lose patients before the page loads
You have an SSL certificate (https://) — your website URL should start with https, not http. Google penalizes non-secure sites.
Your booking button is visible above the fold — patients should never have to scroll to find how to book
High-impact content fixes
Build a dedicated FAQ page — using the 40 questions in Module 3 — organized by service category
Add an "About" page with provider credentials — full name, license type, state, training, and a real photo. Patients check credentials before booking.
Add before/after photos to every service page — not just a gallery page — inline photos on the service page convert significantly better
Embed your Google reviews on your homepage — use Elfsight or your website platform's review widget — social proof on the homepage increases conversions
Write meta descriptions for every page — 150–160 characters that include your service and city — this is the text that appears under your link in Google results
Add alt text to every image — describe what's in the photo plus your city — "Botox injection treatment at med spa in Oswego Illinois"
Create a blog or resources section — even 4–6 articles per year significantly boosts SEO — topics: "How to prepare for Botox," "What is the Myers' Cocktail," etc.
Local SEO extras — done once, works forever
Submit to local directories — Yelp, Healthgrades, RealSelf, ZocDoc, and your local Chamber of Commerce. Each listing is a backlink that boosts your authority.
Add Schema markup to your website — tell your web developer to add LocalBusiness schema — this helps Google understand exactly what you are and where you're located
Embed a Google Map on your contact page — boosts local relevance signals and helps patients find you
Add an Instagram feed to your website — keeps your site fresh with new content automatically and shows social proof
Set up Google Search Console — free tool that shows you which searches bring people to your site, and what pages Google has indexed
Set up Google Analytics 4 — free tool that shows how many people visit your site, where they come from, and what pages they look at
Add a "Book Now" button to your website header that's visible on mobile — the single highest-converting change most med spa websites can make
Compress all images before uploading — use tinypng.com — large images are the #1 cause of slow websites

Start with the top 8 — this week

Don't try to fix all 25 at once. The top 8 critical fixes — page titles, individual service pages, your city in the homepage, consistent NAP, mobile-friendly, fast loading, HTTPS, and a visible booking button — will produce 80% of your SEO improvement. Do those first. Then work through the rest over 30 days.
