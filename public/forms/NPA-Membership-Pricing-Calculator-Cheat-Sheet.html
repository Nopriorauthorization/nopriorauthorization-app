Membership Revenue Math — Why It Changes Everything
Without membership (transactional)Patient books when they want to. Average 3–4 visits per year. Average transaction $250. Annual LTV: $750–1,000
With membership (recurring)Patient commits monthly. 12 guaranteed visits minimum. Monthly $199 = $2,388/year. LTV increases 2–3x immediately
50 members at $199/month$9,950/month guaranteed recurring revenue — regardless of slow seasons, holidays, or cancellations. This is your floor.
100 members at $199/month$19,900/month recurring. Add service revenue on top of memberships = significantly stronger business
Breakeven logicIf your monthly overhead is $15,000 and you have 75 members at $199/month ($14,925) — your membership base nearly covers all overhead. Every service is profit.
Membership vs Package — Key Differences
Membership (Recurring Monthly)
Auto-charges monthly — predictable revenue
Does not accumulate — use it or lose it (prevents backlog)
12-month commitment or month-to-month with cancellation policy
Keeps patient coming in every month — drives retention
Upsell opportunities every visit
Best for services with monthly use cases (Botox, facials, IV)
Package (Prepaid Bundle)
Patient pays upfront — immediate cash but creates liability
Services accumulate if not used — backlog can hurt cash flow
No recurring obligation — patient may disappear after using package
Lower retention rate than membership
Good for: laser series, peel series, one-time treatment plans
Liability note: prepaid services are refundable in many states
Membership Agreement Essentials
Commitment term12-month commitment OR month-to-month with 30-day cancellation notice required. Annual always better for business.
Auto-renewal clauseAutomatically renews unless cancelled 30 days before renewal date. State clearly in agreement.
Non-transferableMembership benefits belong to the member only — cannot be shared or transferred to family members (unless explicitly structured)
Use-it-or-lose-itMonthly inclusions do not roll over. This prevents backlog and maintains revenue predictability. State clearly.
Pause policyAllow 1–2 pause periods per year (medical, travel). No more — prevents abuse. Frozen months do not count toward commitment.
Cancellation feeIf patient cancels before 12-month term: remaining months × 50% OR a flat cancellation fee. Disclose clearly at enrollment.
Membership Launch Strategy
★ Launch with a founding member rate (10–15% below your future rate) — creates urgency and rewards early adopters
★ Cap founding members at 50–100 people — scarcity creates urgency and allows you to test the economics before scaling
★ Present membership at checkout after a great result — "the best time to ask is when they're looking in the mirror happy"
★ Train every staff member to mention the membership. If only you know about it, only you will sell it.
★ Track monthly: new enrollments, cancellations, net member count, average tier value, monthly recurring revenue. These are your most important business metrics.
