What is CJC-1295

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin is one of the most popular and well-studied growth hormone peptide stacks in aesthetic medicine. CJC-1295 is a GHRH analog that provides a sustained increase in baseline growth hormone levels, while Ipamorelin is a selective GH secretagogue that triggers a strong, clean GH pulse without raising cortisol or prolactin. Together they create a synergistic dual-action effect — elevating both baseline and pulsatile GH release for maximum body composition, recovery, and anti-aging benefit.

How It Works
🧠

Dual GH Stimulation

CJC-1295 extends GH half-life for sustained elevation; Ipamorelin triggers a strong pulsatile GH release — together maximizing total GH output.

💪

Anabolic Signaling

Elevated GH increases IGF-1 — driving muscle protein synthesis, fat metabolism, and tissue repair.

🌙

Nocturnal GH Pulse

Dosed at bedtime, the stack amplifies the natural GH surge during deep sleep — critical for recovery and cellular repair.

🎯

Selective & Clean

Ipamorelin raises GH without increasing cortisol, prolactin, or appetite hormones — one of the safest GH secretagogues available.

What You Need to Know

Important before your treatment:

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin is injected subcutaneously — most commonly at bedtime to align with your body's natural GH release.
Do not eat for 2 hours before your injection — food raises insulin and significantly blunts GH release.
IGF-1 blood levels are monitored to optimize your dose and confirm therapeutic GH elevation.
Side effects are generally mild: injection site redness, mild water retention early in treatment, occasional tingling or flushing.
Contraindications: active cancer, pituitary tumors, diabetic retinopathy, pregnancy. Always disclose full medical history.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why combine CJC-1295 with Ipamorelin?
Each peptide works through a different mechanism. CJC-1295 provides sustained GH elevation; Ipamorelin triggers a selective GH pulse. Together they produce significantly better results than either alone.
How long until I see results?
Improved sleep within 1–2 weeks. Body composition changes begin at 6–8 weeks and peak at 3–6 months of consistent use.
Is this the same as taking HGH?
No. This stack stimulates your pituitary to produce its own GH — it does not introduce synthetic hormone. Safer and more physiologically balanced than exogenous HGH.
Do I need blood tests?
Yes. IGF-1 levels are checked before starting and periodically during treatment to confirm optimal dosing.
Can I combine with other peptides?
Yes — commonly combined with BPC-157 for recovery, GHK-Cu for skin, or PT-141 for sexual health. Your provider will design the right stack for your goals.
How long do I stay on it?
Most protocols run 3–6 month cycles followed by a break. Long-term use is monitored with periodic labs.
