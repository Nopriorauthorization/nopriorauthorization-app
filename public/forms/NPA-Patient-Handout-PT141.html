What is PT-141

PT-141 (Bremelanotide) is a synthetic peptide that activates melanocortin receptors in the brain — specifically targeting the neural pathways responsible for sexual arousal and desire. Unlike medications such as Viagra or Cialis that work on blood flow, PT-141 works centrally in the brain — addressing low libido and sexual dysfunction at its neurological root. It is FDA-approved as Vyleesi for hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD) in premenopausal women, and is widely used off-label for both men and women experiencing low sexual desire, arousal difficulties, or sexual dysfunction.

How It Works
🧠

Central Nervous System Action

PT-141 activates melanocortin MC3R and MC4R receptors in the hypothalamus — the brain's sexual arousal center — increasing desire and arousal neurologically.

💕

Increases Sexual Desire

Directly increases libido by activating pathways that generate natural sexual interest — not just physical response, but genuine desire.

🩸

Improves Arousal Response

Enhances physical arousal responses in both men and women — supporting natural lubrication, sensitivity, and erectile function.

⚖️

Addresses Root Cause

Many sexual dysfunction cases are neurological or hormonal — PT-141 addresses the brain-based component that other medications do not target.

What You Need to Know

Important before your treatment:

PT-141 is administered subcutaneously — typically in the abdomen or thigh — approximately 45 minutes before sexual activity.
Common side effects include facial flushing, nausea, and transient elevated blood pressure. These are dose-dependent and typically mild.
Blood pressure should be checked before each use. PT-141 should not be used in patients with uncontrolled high blood pressure.
Do not use more than once every 24 hours. Do not combine with medications for erectile dysfunction without provider guidance.
Contraindications: uncontrolled hypertension, cardiovascular disease. Disclose all medications — particularly antihypertensives — to your provider.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is PT-141 administered?
PT-141 is injected subcutaneously — in the abdomen or thigh — approximately 45–90 minutes before anticipated sexual activity. Your provider will train you on self-administration.
How is PT-141 different from Viagra or Cialis?
Viagra and Cialis work on blood flow to produce physical response. PT-141 works in the brain — increasing actual desire and arousal, not just physical mechanics. They can be complementary.
What are the most common side effects?
Facial flushing and mild nausea are the most common — typically lasting 1–2 hours. These lessen with repeated use. A temporary blood pressure increase may occur.
Can women use PT-141?
Yes — PT-141 is FDA-approved (as Vyleesi) specifically for women with HSDD. It is effective for both premenopausal and postmenopausal women and is widely used off-label.
Can PT-141 be combined with hormone therapy?
Yes — PT-141 and hormone therapy are highly complementary. Hormone therapy addresses hormonal drivers of low libido; PT-141 addresses the neurological component. Many patients benefit from both.
How often can I use PT-141?
No more than once every 24 hours. Most patients use it on an as-needed basis before sexual activity rather than on a scheduled daily protocol.
