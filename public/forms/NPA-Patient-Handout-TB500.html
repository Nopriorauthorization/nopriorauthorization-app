What is TB-500

TB-500 is a synthetic version of Thymosin Beta-4 — a naturally occurring peptide found in high concentrations in wound fluid and healing tissue throughout the body. TB-500 promotes healing by stimulating actin — the protein responsible for cell movement, tissue repair, and wound healing. It has powerful anti-inflammatory, regenerative, and cardioprotective effects, and is widely used in aesthetic and wellness medicine for musculoskeletal recovery, injury healing, and overall cellular repair. It is frequently combined with BPC-157 for a comprehensive recovery stack.

How It Works
🔄

Stimulates Actin Upregulation

TB-500 promotes actin synthesis — essential for cell migration, proliferation, and differentiation needed for tissue repair and wound healing.

🩸

Promotes Angiogenesis

Stimulates new blood vessel formation in healing tissue — improving nutrient and oxygen delivery for faster, more complete recovery.

🔥

Powerful Anti-Inflammatory

Reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines and inflammatory signaling — decreasing pain and swelling in both acute injury and chronic inflammation.

❤️

Cardioprotective Effects

Studied for cardiac muscle repair and protection — TB-500 has demonstrated regenerative effects in cardiac tissue following injury.

What You Need to Know

Important before your treatment:

TB-500 is a research peptide not currently FDA-approved. It is used off-label under provider supervision as an investigational therapy.
Administration is typically subcutaneous injection. Your provider will determine the appropriate dose and injection site based on your injury and goals.
TB-500 is most effective when combined with BPC-157 — together they address complementary healing mechanisms for comprehensive tissue repair.
Side effects are generally mild and rare — may include mild fatigue, dizziness, or injection site reactions.
Disclose all conditions including active cancer, pregnancy, or autoimmune disease before starting. Always use under licensed provider supervision.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is TB-500 administered?
TB-500 is injected subcutaneously — typically at a site near the injury for musculoskeletal conditions, or in the abdomen for systemic use. Your provider determines the appropriate injection protocol.
How does TB-500 compare to BPC-157?
Both are powerful healing peptides but work through different mechanisms. BPC-157 is stronger for gut healing and angiogenesis; TB-500 has broader anti-inflammatory and cardiac effects. They are highly complementary and often stacked.
How long until I see results?
Anti-inflammatory effects often noticed within days. Structural tissue repair takes 4–12 weeks depending on injury severity. Chronic conditions may require longer protocols.
Can TB-500 help with chronic tendon injuries?
Yes — TB-500 is particularly effective for chronic tendinopathy, plantar fasciitis, and other connective tissue conditions that have been resistant to conventional treatment.
Is TB-500 safe long-term?
TB-500 has an excellent safety profile in research. Most protocols cycle 4–8 weeks on with a break. Long-term safety data in humans is limited — use under provider supervision.
Can TB-500 be combined with BPC-157?
Yes — the BPC-157 + TB-500 stack is one of the most popular and effective recovery combinations. They work synergistically through complementary healing mechanisms.
