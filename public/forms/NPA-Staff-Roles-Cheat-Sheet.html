Front Desk — Core Responsibilities
Booking & schedulingManages all appointment booking, cancellations, and rescheduling. Enforces cancellation policy and collects deposits/card on file.
Patient intakeChecks patients in, verifies intake forms and consent forms are signed before treatment begins. Never allows treatment without complete documentation.
Phones & communicationAnswers all incoming calls. Returns messages within 2–4 hours business hours. Routes clinical questions to provider — never gives clinical advice.
Payment processingProcesses all payments (cash, card, CareCredit, Cherry). Issues receipts. Manages end-of-day reconciliation.
Retail salesPresents retail products at checkout. Knows the products well enough to support the provider's recommendations. Tracks retail inventory.
Social media supportTakes before/after photos (with consent), helps with content capture, manages review requests.
Office managementOrders non-clinical supplies. Manages waiting area. Maintains cleanliness and professional appearance of front desk area.
What Front Desk Must NEVER Do
❌ Give clinical advice or answer clinical questions — always route to provider ("Great question, let me have [provider] call you back")
❌ Quote dosing, unit amounts, or product names for clinical services — always quote prices only
❌ Discuss a patient's treatment with anyone other than the patient (HIPAA) — "I'm not able to share any patient information"
❌ Upsell or recommend specific clinical treatments — that is the provider's role at consultation
❌ Access clinical systems or patient charts without specific authorization and training
Licensed Esthetician — Core Responsibilities
Scope of practicePerforms services within licensed esthetic scope: facials, chemical peels (scope-appropriate), microneedling (scope-appropriate per state), waxing, lash, brow, skin consultations.
Patient educationEducates patients on skincare routines, home care, pre/post treatment care. Does NOT give medical advice or discuss injectable treatments clinically.
Room setup and breakdownPrepares treatment rooms before every appointment. Maintains sterile technique and infection control standards. Breaks down room and restocks after each patient.
Product recommendationsRecommends retail skincare products aligned with provider's clinical recommendations. Builds retail relationships with patients.
Before/after photosTakes consistent clinical photos pre and post esthetic treatments per photo system protocol. Obtains and documents photo consent.
ChartingDocuments treatments, products used, patient response, and follow-up plan in chart within 24 hours of service.
Injector Assistant — Core Responsibilities
Room prepPrepares treatment rooms for injectable procedures: sets up sterile field, draws up medications (if licensed and trained), positions patient, labels syringes.
During treatment supportAssists provider during treatment: passes supplies, manages patient comfort, applies topical numbing, monitors patient response.
Supply managementTracks injectable supply inventory. Orders clinical supplies. Manages medication storage (refrigeration, controlled substances log if applicable).
Post-treatment careProvides post-treatment instructions to patients. Applies cold compress, takes post-treatment photos, schedules follow-up.
Biohazard managementDisposes of sharps in approved sharps containers. Manages biohazard waste disposal. Maintains OSHA compliance documentation.
Scope limitsInjector assistants who are not independently licensed providers do NOT perform injections, draw conclusions about clinical outcomes, or advise patients on treatment plans.
Universal Communication Protocols — All Staff
Always Say
"Let me get [provider] to answer that for you"
"I'll have someone follow up with you within [timeframe]"
"Thank you so much for choosing us"
"Is there anything else I can help you with today?"
"We'd love to see you back — can I schedule your next visit?"
"I'm not able to share that information, but [provider] would be happy to discuss it with you"
Never Say
"That treatment doesn't really work for..."
"Honestly, I think you should..."
"I know [provider] usually does..."
"We've had patients complain about..."
"Between us..."
"I don't know" (without following up immediately)
"That's not my job"
