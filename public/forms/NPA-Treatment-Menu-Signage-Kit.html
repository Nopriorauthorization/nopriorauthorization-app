What to Expect After

Results in aesthetic medicine take time — and that's not a flaw, it's how it works.

  • Botox: visible in 3–7 days, full result at day 14
  • Filler: swelling resolves over 1–2 weeks, final result at 4 weeks
  • Microneedling: collagen builds over 3–6 months post-series
  • Chemical peels: visible improvement begins at week 2
  • GLP-1: therapeutic weight loss begins weeks 4–8

We'll follow up with you — you're never on your own after you leave.

Photo consent display — in every treatment room

A Note About Photos

We take before and after photos at every visit — here's why, and what that means for you

Before-and-after photos are one of the most important parts of your care here. They give us a clinical baseline, allow us to track your progress over time, and help you see what we see — which is often more than you notice on your own.

Your photos are stored securely in your patient record and are never shared, posted, or used in any way without your explicit written consent. We take your privacy as seriously as we take your results.

If you would like your photos used for educational or marketing purposes, we will ask you to sign a separate photo release — and we will always show you the photo before it goes anywhere. Your comfort and consent are not optional steps. They are the starting point.

If you have any questions about how your photos are stored or used, please ask us — we are always happy to explain.

Why this sign matters clinically and legally

The marketing photo release — a separate document

Your Canva kit includes a photo release template. It covers: permission to use photos on social media, website, and marketing materials; permission to tag or not tag the patient; duration of consent; and the patient's right to revoke consent in writing. Keep a signed copy in every patient chart. This is the document that lets you post your best before/afters legally and confidently.
