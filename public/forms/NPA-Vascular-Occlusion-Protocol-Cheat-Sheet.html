THE 7-STEP PROTOCOL — Execute Immediately
STEP 1 — STOPPut the syringe down immediately. Do not inject another drop. Assess the patient and skin now.
STEP 2 — HYALURONIDASEInject 150–300 units (Hylenex preferred) throughout affected zone AND along vessel course. Do not wait. Have it in the room — always.
STEP 3 — MASSAGE + HEATVigorous massage over affected area. Apply warm compress immediately to promote vasodilation. Continue throughout.
STEP 4 — ASPIRINChew (not swallow whole) 325mg aspirin immediately if no contraindication. Antiplatelet effect reduces clot extension.
STEP 5 — CHECK VISION"Is your vision normal in both eyes?" Any visual change = CALL 911 NOW. Retinal artery occlusion is irreversible within 90 minutes.
STEP 6 — REPEAT HYAL q60 minIf blanching/discoloration not improving at 60 min — repeat hyaluronidase 150u. Follow up every 24 hrs until resolved.
STEP 7 — CALL CARRIERContact your malpractice carrier as soon as patient is stabilized. Document everything — product, lot, volume, points, timeline.

Post-Event Documentation — Document All of This
📋 PRODUCT: Exact product name, lot number, expiration date, volume injected, injection points, depths
📋 TIMELINE: Time of injection, first symptom, each intervention, each reassessment — minute by minute
📋 PHOTOS: At event, each treatment interval, 24-hr follow-up, resolution or referral
📋 TREATMENT: Hyaluronidase doses and timing, aspirin, warm compress, massage, any other interventions
📋 COMMUNICATION: What you told patient, instructions given, referrals made, emergency services contacted
📋 CARRIER: When you contacted malpractice carrier and what was communicated
