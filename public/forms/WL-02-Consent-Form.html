GLP-1 Weight Loss Therapy
Informed Consent
Semaglutide · Tirzepatide · Liraglutide
GLP-1 receptor agonists are medications that regulate appetite, blood sugar, and digestion. This consent explains the risks, benefits, and what's expected of you.
Expected Benefits
Average 10–20%+ weight loss over 12–18 months in clinical trials
Improvement in blood sugar, blood pressure, and cholesterol
Reduced appetite and improved satiety
Potential improvement in obesity-related conditions

Individual results vary significantly. Weight loss is not guaranteed.

Common Side Effects
Nausea (most common, especially early) · Vomiting · Diarrhea or constipation · Decreased appetite · Fatigue · Dizziness · Heartburn / acid reflux · Headache · Injection site reactions · Hair loss (often temporary)
Serious Risks (Rare)
Black Box Warning: In animal studies, GLP-1 medications caused thyroid C-cell tumors. Contraindicated in patients with personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia type 2 (MEN2).
Pancreatitis (stop medication and seek care if severe abdominal pain) · Gallbladder disease · Kidney injury (stay hydrated) · Vision changes (monitor closely with diabetic retinopathy)
Contraindications
Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2
Pregnancy or breastfeeding
Known hypersensitivity to GLP-1 medications
Severe gastrointestinal disease including gastroparesis
