Insurance Disclaimer
Cosmetic Services Are Not Covered by Insurance All procedures and services performed at Hello Gorgeous Med Spa are cosmetic and elective in nature. We do not accept, bill, or file claims with any health insurance provider. The patient is responsible for the full cost of all services, consultations, products, and follow-up care. If you believe a service may be covered by your insurance, you are responsible for verifying coverage and submitting claims independently.
Outstanding Balances & Collections

Accounts with balances past due for 30 days or more may be referred to a third-party collections agency. In such cases, the patient is responsible for all costs of collection, including but not limited to agency fees, court costs, and reasonable attorney's fees. We encourage patients to communicate proactively about any billing concerns.

Returned Payments

A fee of $35.00 will be assessed for any returned check or declined electronic payment. Future payments may be required in cash or certified funds following a returned payment. The returned payment fee is in addition to any outstanding balance owed for services.
