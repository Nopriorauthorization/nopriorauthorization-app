Your First Visit
Your guide to a great experience
We want you to feel prepared and at ease from the moment you walk in. This guide will help you know exactly what to bring, what to expect, and how to get the most out of your first appointment.
Arrival Checklist — What to Bring

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled appointment to allow time for check-in. Bring the following items:

Valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport)
Insurance card, if applicable
Completed intake forms (if not submitted online)
List of current medications, vitamins, and supplements
List of known allergies (medications, latex, topicals)
Preferred payment method (card, cash, or financing info)

Arriving without required items may delay your appointment. Please come with a clean face free of makeup if your visit involves facial assessment.

Your Consultation — Step by Step
1

Welcome & Check-In — Our front desk team will greet you, verify your information, and ensure your paperwork is complete. You will be escorted to a private treatment room.

2

Meet Your Provider — Your provider will introduce themselves, review your health history, and take time to listen to your concerns, goals, and expectations.

3

Assessment — A thorough evaluation of the treatment area will be performed. This may include skin analysis, measurements, or clinical photography for your medical record.

4

Treatment Options — Your provider will explain all appropriate treatment options, including benefits, risks, expected outcomes, recovery time, and costs. We believe in complete transparency.

5

Personalized Plan — Together, you and your provider will create a customized treatment plan tailored to your goals, timeline, and budget. There is never any pressure to proceed.

Treatment Planning

After your consultation, your provider may recommend a treatment plan that includes:

ComponentDetails
Recommended TreatmentsSpecific procedures and products tailored to your goals and anatomy
Treatment TimelineSequencing of treatments for optimal results — some may be performed same-day, others require spacing
Expected ResultsRealistic expectations for improvement, including gradual versus immediate results
Home CareSkincare regimen, pre- and post-treatment instructions, and product recommendations to support your results
Cost EstimateTransparent pricing for all recommended services, with financing options if desired
Timeline Expectations
Immediate Results Some treatments, such as dermal fillers and certain laser procedures, show visible improvement right away. However, final results develop over days to weeks as swelling subsides and tissues settle.
Gradual Results Neurotoxins typically take 7–14 days for full effect. Skin rejuvenation, chemical peels, and collagen-stimulating treatments often require a series of sessions over weeks or months for optimal outcomes.
Please remember: Individual results vary based on anatomy, skin type, age, lifestyle, and treatment adherence. Your provider will give you a realistic timeline during your consultation.
Follow-Up & Ongoing Care

Follow-up appointments are an important part of your treatment journey:

• Why it matters: Follow-ups allow your provider to assess results, make adjustments, and plan next steps for your care.
• When to schedule: Your provider will recommend a follow-up timeline based on your specific treatment — typically 2–4 weeks post-procedure.
• How to schedule: Book at checkout, call us at 630-636-6193, or use our online booking portal.
• Concerns between visits: If you experience unexpected side effects or have questions before your follow-up, do not hesitate to call our office. We are here for you.

When to Contact Us vs. Seek Emergency Care
Call Our Office (630-636-6193):
• Unusual swelling, bruising, or discomfort
• Questions about post-treatment care
• Signs of minor infection (redness, warmth)
• Concerns about your results
• Medication or product questions
Go to the ER or Call 911:
• Difficulty breathing or swallowing
• Severe allergic reaction (hives, throat tightening)
• Vision changes after facial treatment
• Signs of vascular compromise (blanching, severe pain)
• Chest pain or signs of anaphylaxis
